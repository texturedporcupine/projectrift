# SF Bay Area Communities — Direct Mail Guide

## How to Use This Guide

Each community profile follows the same structure: tier classification, key demographics, community character, EDDM routing notes, and recommended advertiser categories. Use this guide to select campaign targets, build prospect lists, and customize pitch decks.

---

## Tier Definitions

| Tier | Characteristics | Standard Ad Price | Premium Placement |
|------|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1 — Premium** | Median HHI $185K+, established prestige, strong HOA culture | $700–900 | $1,100–1,400 |
| **Tier 2 — Strong** | Median HHI $140K–185K, high homeownership, growing commercial corridors | $550–750 | $850–1,100 |
| **Tier 3 — Growth** | Median HHI $115K–145K, newer developments, high household density | $400–600 | $650–850 |

## Community Profiles

### 1. Danville, CA — 94526

**Tier:** 1 — Premium
**Median HHI:** $185,000+
**Households:** 18,000+
**Homeownership:** Very high (~85%)

**Community Character:**
Danville is the jewel of the San Ramon Valley — an affluent, walkable downtown with a strong "small town in the suburbs" identity. Residents are highly engaged with community events and local governance. The active Town of Danville HOA network and close-knit neighborhood culture means direct mail gets read here, not tossed. Think of it as the Bay Area's equivalent of Circle C Ranch in Austin.

**Key Commercial Corridors:**
- Hartz Avenue downtown corridor (restaurants, boutiques, professional services)
- Diablo Road / Sycamore Valley Road (dental, medical, home services)
- Crow Canyon Road strip (HVAC, landscaping, auto services)

**EDDM Routing Notes:**
Enter zip 94526 on eddm.usps.com. Filter: HHI $100K+, household size 2.5+. Target 2–3 carrier routes covering Alamo-adjacent and central Danville neighborhoods. Expect 5,000–7,000 qualifying households across selected routes.

**Recommended Categories (8–10):**
1. HVAC / heating & air conditioning
2. Landscaping & lawn care
3. Pool service & repair
4. General dentistry
5. Med spa / aesthetics
6. House cleaning / maid service
7. Plumbing
8. Roofing
9. Pest control
10. Interior design / home staging

**Why Start Here:**
Strongest community identity + mail-reading culture + high density of affluent homeowners + thriving local business ecosystem. Best first campaign target.

**Tier:** 1–2 (routes vary by neighborhood)

San Ramon is a master-planned suburban city anchored by the Bishop Ranch business park and Dougherty Valley developments. **EDDM Routing Notes:**
Two zip codes cover San Ramon. 94583 covers central/older San Ramon; 94582 covers Dougherty Valley and newer developments. Use both for full coverage or pick one for a focused 5,000-household campaign.

**Recommended Categories (8–10):**

2. Tutoring / test prep (Kumon, Mathnasium-style)
3. Family / general dentistry
4. Pest control
5. Landscaping & tree service
6. Pool service
7. House cleaning
8. Pediatric dentistry / orthodontics
9. Home remodeling / kitchen & bath
10. Insurance (home/auto bundle)

### 4. Los Gatos, CA — 95030 / 95032

**Tier:** 1 — Premium
**Median HHI:** $200,000+
**Households:** 12,000+
**Homeownership:** High (~70%)

**Community Character:**
Los Gatos is one of the most affluent communities in the South Bay — a hillside town with a walkable, upscale downtown and strong local identity. Residents are discerning consumers who value quality and exclusivity. The community has a "village" feel despite proximity to San Jose. Excellent fit for premium-positioned services and luxury home categories.

**Key Commercial Corridors:**
- North Santa Cruz Avenue downtown (restaurants, boutiques, wine bars)
- Los Gatos Blvd / Blossom Hill area (dental, medical, fitness)
- Highway 9 corridor toward Saratoga (home services, landscaping)

**EDDM Routing Notes:**
95030 covers downtown and central Los Gatos; 95032 extends toward the eastern hillside neighborhoods. For a premium-tier campaign, focus on 95030 routes. Smaller total household count means you may want only 2 carrier routes.

**Recommended Categories (8–10):**
1. Med spa / aesthetics / skin care
2. Luxury home renovation / custom cabinetry
3. Pool service & repair (many hillside pools)
4. General / cosmetic dentistry
5. Landscaping / hardscaping
6. House cleaning (premium residential)
7. Interior design / home staging
8. Financial planning / wealth management
9. Wine cellar / specialty home features
10. HVAC (premium systems)

### 5. Saratoga, CA — 95070

**Tier:** 1 — Premium
**Median HHI:** $250,000+
**Households:** 10,000+
**Homeownership:** Very high (~85%)

**Community Character:**
Saratoga is one of the highest-income communities in the entire Bay Area. Large-lot single-family homes, excellent schools, and a quiet, privacy-focused residential culture. Residents tend to be established professionals and executives. The local business ecosystem caters to luxury: estate services, premium landscaping, concierge medicine. Lower household density means campaigns target fewer but higher-value routes.

**Key Commercial Corridors:**
- Big Basin Way / Saratoga Village (restaurants, boutiques, specialty retail)
- Saratoga-Sunnyvale Road (professional services, medical)
- Prospect Road corridor (home services)

**EDDM Routing Notes:**
Single zip code (95070). Filter for HHI $150K+ to isolate the core affluent neighborhoods. Total qualifying households may be 4,000–6,000 — perfect for a single focused campaign.

**Recommended Categories (6–8):**
1. Luxury home renovation / estate remodeling
2. Med spa / concierge dermatology
3. Estate planning / wealth management
4. Premium landscaping / arborist services
5. House cleaning (estate-level)
6. General / cosmetic dentistry
7. Pool service (estate pools)
8. Security / smart home systems

### 6. Los Altos / Los Altos Hills, CA — 94022 / 94024

**Tier:** 1 — Premium
**Median HHI:** $300,000+ (Hills), $220,000+ (Los Altos proper)
**Households:** 8,000+ (combined)
**Homeownership:** Very high (~90% in Hills)

**Community Character:**
Los Altos Hills is among the wealthiest zip codes in the nation — large properties, often on acre+ lots, with a rural-suburban feel. Los Altos proper has a walkable downtown and more traditional suburban neighborhoods. Both communities have exceptionally high disposable income and demand for premium home services.

**Key Commercial Corridors:**
- Main Street, Los Altos (downtown — restaurants, shops, services)
- El Camino Real near Los Altos (dental, medical, professional)
- San Antonio Road corridor (services, retail)

**EDDM Routing Notes:**
94022 covers Los Altos proper; 94024 includes Los Altos Hills. The Hills has very low density (large lots), so household counts per route will be small. Consider combining routes from both zip codes.

**Recommended Categories (6–8):**
1. Premium landscaping / garden design
2. Concierge medicine / functional medicine
3. Estate maintenance / property management
4. House cleaning (estate-level)
5. General / cosmetic dentistry
6. Financial advisory / tax planning
7. Pool & spa service
8. Smart home / home automation

**Homeownership:** High (~68%)

**Community Character:**
Dublin is one of the fastest-growing cities in the East Bay — master-planned developments, new schools, and a rapidly expanding commercial base near the BART station. ### 11. Brentwood, CA — 94513

**Tier:** 3 — Growth
**Median HHI:** $115,000+
**Households:** 22,000+
**Homeownership:** Very high (~82%)

**Community Character:**
Brentwood is a rapidly growing East Contra Costa community that has transitioned from agricultural to suburban over the past 15 years. Many master-planned communities (Trilogy, Summerset, Shadow Lakes) with active HOAs. Residents are value-conscious families who moved for affordability relative to inner Bay Area. High homeownership and newer homes create strong demand for home services.

**Key Commercial Corridors:**
- Lone Tree Way corridor (restaurants, retail, services — main drag)
- Sand Creek Road / Balfour Road (newer commercial centers)
- Downtown Brentwood (smaller shops, professional services)

**EDDM Routing Notes:**
Single large zip code. High household density in master-planned communities. Filter for HHI $100K+ and household size 2.5+. Easily achieve 6,000–8,000 households with 2–3 routes.

**Recommended Categories (8–10):**
1. Pool service & repair (nearly universal in new developments)
2. HVAC / heating & cooling
3. Pest control (agricultural fringe = more pest issues)
4. Roofing (newer construction but sun exposure)
5. Landscaping & lawn care
6. General dentistry
7. House cleaning
8. Plumbing
9. Garage door repair
10. Solar panel maintenance

### 12. Rocklin / Roseville, CA — 95765 / 95677

**Tier:** 3 — Growth
**Median HHI:** $120,000+
**Households:** 35,000+ (combined)
**Homeownership:** High (~74%)

**Community Character:**
Rocklin and Roseville sit in the Sacramento metro's western growth corridor — massive master-planned communities (West Roseville, Whitney Ranch, Stanford Ranch) with young families. While technically outside the Bay Area proper, commuter patterns and demographic profiles make them viable expansion markets. Very high household density and active HOAs make direct mail effective here.

**Key Commercial Corridors:**
- Granite Drive / Rocklin Road (Rocklin commercial center)
- Douglas Blvd / Roseville Parkway (Roseville's main retail corridors)
- Pleasant Grove / Blue Oaks Blvd (West Roseville newer commercial)

**EDDM Routing Notes:**
Multiple zip codes serve this area. Start with 95765 (west Rocklin) or 95677 (central Roseville) for concentrated household density. Enormous total addressable market — could support multiple campaigns.

**Recommended Categories (8–10):**

## Community Research Checklist (run for each new community)

- [ ] Go to eddm.usps.com → enter target zip → filter HHI $100K+, household size 2.5+
- [ ] Screenshot 2–3 viable routes, note household counts
- [ ] Search "[community] HOA" — confirm active HOA exists
- [ ] Search "[community] community newsletter" — check Nextdoor, local papers, HOA newsletter
- [ ] Use Google Maps to identify commercial corridors serving the neighborhood
- [ ] Build 30–40 business prospect list across 8–10 non-competing categories
- [ ] Verify EDDM carrier route boundaries match target neighborhood
- [ ] Confirm DDU (Destination Delivery Unit) location for drop-off
