## 4. Design Requirements Per Ad Space

Each advertiser's ad space must include:
- Business name and logo
- Phone number (primary CTA)
- Website URL (secondary CTA)
- Special offer or call-to-action (e.g., "Free Estimate," "$50 Off First Service")
- Tagline or brief description (1–2 lines max)

Design guidelines:
- Use advertiser's brand colors where possible
- Solid hex fills only (no transparency on dark backgrounds — pptxgenjs quirk)
- Minimum font size: 8pt for body, 12pt for headlines
- High-contrast text on all backgrounds
- All images must be 300 DPI minimum for print

---

## 5. EDDM Requirements Checklist

- [ ] Postcard meets USPS flat-mail dimensions (>6.125" tall OR >10.5" long)
- [ ] 14pt cardstock (0.007" minimum thickness)
- [ ] EDDM indicia placed in upper-right corner of address side
- [ ] "Local Postal Customer" or "Postal Customer" address line
- [ ] Return address in upper-left corner
- [ ] No individual addressing (EDDM = every door)
- [ ] Postcards bundled by carrier route (face up, address side visible)
- [ ] EDDM paperwork completed at eddm.usps.com
- [ ] Postage paid at DDU: $0.247/piece
