# Email 3 — Day 7 Value-Add (Final Touch)

**Subject Line Options:**
- "Last call — [CATEGORY] spot in [COMMUNITY] campaign closing soon"
- "A quick thought for [BUSINESS NAME]"
- "Before I lock in another [CATEGORY] provider for [COMMUNITY]..."

---

**From:** [YOUR NAME], Bay Area Direct Mail Co.
**To:** [CONTACT NAME], [BUSINESS NAME]

---

Hi [CONTACT FIRST NAME],

This is my last note on this — I don't want to be a pest.

I'm finalizing the advertiser lineup for our [COMMUNITY] direct mail campaign ([CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households, [COMMUNITY ZIP]) and the [CATEGORY] spot is still open. I wanted to give you one more look before I reach out to your competitors in the area.

**Here's what I want you to consider:**

Imagine [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homeowners in [COMMUNITY] — median income [MEDIAN HHI] — pulling your postcard out of their mailbox this month. Your business name, your phone number, your offer, right there in their hands. No scrolling past. No clicking away. No algorithm deciding whether they see you.

And you're the **only** [CATEGORY] option on the card.

**What's included in your ad space:**
- Professional ad design (we handle layout and creative)
- Full proof approval before printing
- USPS EDDM delivery to every selected household
- Category exclusivity — no competitors on the same card
- Post-campaign delivery confirmation

**Investment:** [STANDARD PRICE] for standard | [PREMIUM PRICE] for premium placement

If you're interested, I just need a quick 10-minute call to walk through the details and reserve your spot. If it's not the right fit, no worries at all — I appreciate your time.

Either way, let me know and I'll update my list.

Best,
[YOUR NAME]
Bay Area Direct Mail Co.
[PHONE]
[EMAIL]

P.S. — We offer a 15–20% discount for businesses that commit to 2 or more campaigns. If you'd rather test with one and see results first, that works too.

---

**Personalization Notes:**
- This is the urgency/scarcity email — emphasize the category exclusivity and the fact you'll move on to competitors
- If you've spoken to or emailed with competitors, you can reference it: "I've had conversations with two other [CATEGORY] providers in the area, but I wanted to give you first right of refusal."
- Keep the tone respectful — "last call" not "last chance"
- The P.S. works well for the discount hook — people read the P.S.
