# Pitch Script — Plumbing

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. I'm putting together an exclusive advertising campaign for [COMMUNITY] — [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homes, [MEDIAN HHI] median income — and I need one plumber. Are you open to hearing how it works? Takes about two minutes."

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"You'd be the only plumbing company on this postcard. When a pipe bursts or a water heater fails in [COMMUNITY], your name is the one sitting on their counter."

**The Emergency Play:**
"Plumbing is the ultimate 'fridge magnet' category. Nobody plans a plumbing emergency. But everyone needs a plumber eventually. The business that's already in their home — physically, on their counter or fridge — gets the call."

**The Numbers:**
"Average plumbing service call is $250–500. A water heater replacement is $2,000–4,000. One emergency call from [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households and your [STANDARD PRICE] ad space is paid for twice over."

**The Neighborhood Fit:**
"[COMMUNITY] has a mix of [HOUSING DESCRIPTION] — all of which need plumbing service. These homeowners don't DIY their plumbing. They call a professional and they pay the going rate."

## Objection Handling

**"We don't do advertising — all word of mouth."**
"Word of mouth is the best, and direct mail actually amplifies it. When your existing customers' neighbors see your name on the postcard, it reinforces the referral. 'Oh yeah, I've heard of them AND I got their postcard.' That's double the trust."

**"We can't handle more volume."**
"Totally understand. But here's the thing — [COMMUNITY] customers tend to be higher-ticket. Better to do fewer jobs at higher margins in one affluent neighborhood than to chase lower-value work across the whole metro."

**"We spent money on Google Ads and got burned."**
"Google Ads for plumbing is cutthroat — $40–60 per click in the Bay Area. Direct mail is a flat fee to reach [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homes. No bidding wars. No click fraud. And the physical postcard has a shelf life — it sits on counters and fridges for weeks."

## Close

"Let me send you the route map for [COMMUNITY] and a sample ad layout. If it looks good, I just need 10 minutes to walk through the details. Fair enough?"
