# Pitch Script — Landscaping & Lawn Care

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. I'm organizing an exclusive direct mail campaign for [COMMUNITY] — [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households — and I'm looking for the right landscaping company to feature. You'd be the only one on the card. Interested in hearing more?"

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"One landscaping company per postcard. That's it. No competing with three other lawn guys on the same mailer."

**The Seasonal Play:**
"Landscaping is one of the strongest direct mail categories because it's visible. Every homeowner looks at their yard, looks at their neighbor's yard, and thinks about what they could improve. Your ad arrives right when that thought is forming."

**The Numbers:**
"A regular maintenance contract averages $200–400/month — that's $2,400–4,800/year. Add in one hardscaping or redesign project at $5,000–15,000 and the ROI from a single postcard campaign at [STANDARD PRICE] is potentially 10x+."

**The Neighborhood Fit:**
"[COMMUNITY] homeowners have [MEDIAN HHI] median income and [HOMEOWNERSHIP RATE] homeownership. These are people who care about curb appeal. HOA-governed communities especially — residents are required to maintain their properties. They need landscapers, and they'll pay for quality."

## Objection Handling

**"We're booked out for months."**
"That tells me your business is solid — and that you should be selective about new clients. Direct mail targeting [COMMUNITY] specifically brings you the high-value clients: bigger properties, bigger budgets, longer-term contracts. Quality over quantity."

**"Our crews cover the whole East Bay."**
"Which means you're driving past [COMMUNITY] every day. Wouldn't it be better to have 5 clients on the same block than 5 clients across 5 cities? This campaign puts your name in front of a concentrated neighborhood of ideal clients."

**"We tried Yelp/Thumbtack and it was expensive."**
"Those platforms charge per lead, and the leads are price-shopping. A postcard in [COMMUNITY] reaches homeowners before they start price-shopping. You're not bidding against 10 competitors — you're the only landscaper they see."

## Close

"I'd love to show you the carrier route map and a sample ad layout. Can I send that over today and follow up with a quick call this week?"
