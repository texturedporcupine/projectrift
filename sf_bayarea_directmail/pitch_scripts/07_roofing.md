# Pitch Script — Roofing

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. I'm running a direct mail campaign targeting [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homeowners in [COMMUNITY] and I'm looking for one roofing company to feature exclusively. Given your work in the area, I thought of you first. Got a couple minutes?"

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"One roofing company per campaign. Period. You'll be the only roofer [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homeowners see."

**The Proactive Play:**
"Most homeowners don't think about their roof until there's a leak. But everyone knows they should get an inspection. A postcard offering a free roof inspection or seasonal check-up gets your foot in the door before the emergency — and positions you for the $10K–30K reroof when the time comes."

**The Numbers:**
"Average roof repair is $500–2,000. A full reroof is $15,000–30,000 in the Bay Area. Your ad space is [STANDARD PRICE]. One repair lead pays for the campaign. One reroof lead pays for an entire year of marketing."

**The Neighborhood Fit:**
"[COMMUNITY] homes range from [HOUSING AGE RANGE]. Older homes need maintenance and replacement. Newer homes need first inspections after their warranty periods. Either way, [MEDIAN HHI] homeowners don't patch their own roofs."

## Objection Handling

**"Roofing is storm-driven / seasonal."**
"That's exactly why proactive marketing works. By the time a storm hits, everyone's calling. The roofer who was already in their mailbox last month gets the first call. Direct mail builds the pipeline before the emergency."

**"We do commercial, not residential."**
"Understood. But [COMMUNITY] also has commercial corridors — HOA clubhouses, small office parks, retail strips. And residential leads from affluent neighborhoods often lead to commercial referrals through business owners who live there."

**"Our average ticket is too high — people need to meet us first."**
"Absolutely, and that's the point. The postcard doesn't close the sale — it starts the conversation. A free inspection offer on the card gets you face-to-face with the homeowner. That's where your sales process takes over."

## Close

"Let me put together a route map and a mock ad for your company. If you like what you see, we can lock in your spot and talk about what offer to feature — maybe a free inspection or a seasonal discount. Sound good?"
