# Pitch Script — Pool Service & Repair

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. We're running a direct mail campaign in [COMMUNITY] — [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homes — and I need one pool service company. Given that you serve the area, I wanted to give you first look. Got a minute?"

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"Only one pool service company on the card. When [COMMUNITY] homeowners get this postcard, you're it."

**The Seasonal Play:**
"Spring is when every pool owner realizes they need service — whether it's an opening, a repair, or switching to a new provider because last year's guy ghosted them. Getting your name in their hands in [CAMPAIGN MONTH] is perfect timing."

**The Numbers:**
"Monthly pool service runs $100–200/month — that's $1,200–2,400/year per customer. One new recurring customer from this campaign and you've paid for your ad space [STANDARD PRICE] several times over. And pool customers stay for years."

**The Neighborhood Fit:**
"[COMMUNITY] has a high concentration of homes with pools — especially in the [SPECIFIC NEIGHBORHOODS] areas. These are homeowners with [MEDIAN HHI] median income who don't want to maintain their own pools. They want a reliable professional."

## Objection Handling

**"I'm already at capacity."**
"I hear that a lot from good pool companies — and it makes sense. But consider this: what if your best route had 3 clients on the same street? Direct mail lets you concentrate customers geographically. Denser routes mean less drive time, more profit."

**"My business is all referral."**
"Referrals are the best lead source, no question. But referrals are slow and unpredictable. One campaign puts your name in [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homes at once. Even if 99% ignore it, that 1% is 50–70 potential leads. And your referral customers will see it too — it reinforces that they made the right choice."

**"Too expensive."**
"If your average monthly service is $150, one new customer is $1,800/year. Your ad space is [STANDARD PRICE]. That's a 3–4x return from a single customer, and pool customers average 3–5 year retention."

## Close

"Let me put together a mockup with your business featured and the EDDM routes showing which [COMMUNITY] neighborhoods we're hitting. I can have that to you tomorrow. Work for you?"
