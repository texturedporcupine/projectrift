# Pitch Script — Pest Control

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. I'm organizing an exclusive advertising campaign for [COMMUNITY] — [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homes — and I need one pest control company. Just one. Is that something worth a couple minutes of your time?"

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"We cap it at one pest control provider per campaign. Whoever takes this spot is the only pest control business that every household on our routes will see."

**The Seasonal Play:**
"Pest control is seasonal gold for direct mail. Spring and fall are when homeowners start seeing ants, spiders, rodents — and they need someone fast. Having your name already in their home when that first ant trail appears is the difference between getting the call and losing it to whoever shows up first on Google."

**The Numbers:**
"Your ad space is [STANDARD PRICE]. Divide that by [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homes and you're looking at about [COST PER HOME] per household. Your average initial service call is what — $150–250? One new customer from this campaign and you're halfway to break-even. Two customers and you're profitable, before you count the recurring revenue from quarterly service plans."

**The Neighborhood Fit:**
"[COMMUNITY] is a homeownership-heavy community — [HOMEOWNERSHIP RATE] own their homes. Homeowners invest in pest control. Renters call their landlord. These are your buyers."

## Objection Handling

**"We get all our leads from Google/HomeAdvisor."**
"And that works — until your cost-per-lead goes up again next quarter. Direct mail is a fixed-cost channel that puts your brand in [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homes at once. It's not about replacing Google — it's about being the name they already trust when they do search."

**"Direct mail doesn't work for pest control."**
"Generic coupon mailers don't work for anyone. What works is a professionally designed postcard with category exclusivity, delivered to a targeted affluent neighborhood via USPS. We're not ValPak — we're a curated, premium card with 6–8 non-competing local businesses."

**"Too expensive."**
"One quarterly pest control plan at $40–50/month is worth $480–600/year in recurring revenue. Sign up 3 households from [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] and you've generated $1,500+ in annual revenue from a [STANDARD PRICE] investment. That's a 3x return in year one alone."

## Close

"I'd love to put together a quick visual of what your ad space would look like and the specific [COMMUNITY] carrier routes we're targeting. Can I send that over and schedule a quick call this week?"
