# Pitch Script — Pediatric Dentistry / Orthodontics

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. We're running a direct mail campaign in [COMMUNITY] — [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households, average household size of 2.5+ — and I'm reserving one spot for a pediatric dentist or orthodontist. You serve a lot of families in this area, right? Got a minute?"

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"One pediatric dental/ortho spot per card. You're the only children's dental provider [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] families will see."

**The Family Play:**
"[COMMUNITY] is a family-first community — strong schools, high household sizes, parents who invest heavily in their kids' health and development. When a family moves to [COMMUNITY], finding a pediatric dentist is on the checklist right behind 'enroll in school.'"

**The Numbers:**
"The average pediatric dental patient is worth $400–600/year. A family with 2–3 kids is $1,000–1,800/year. Orthodontic cases run $5,000–7,000 each. One new patient family more than covers your [STANDARD PRICE] ad space. One ortho case pays for years of advertising."

**The Timing Angle:**
"Back-to-school season and the start of the year are peak times for new patient acquisition. Parents want their kids' dental care sorted before school starts. Timing this campaign right puts your practice in front of families during their decision window."

## Objection Handling

**"We're mostly insurance-driven."**
"Insurance tells families which providers are in-network, but families still choose. When they're comparing three in-network pediatric dentists, the one they've already seen on a postcard feels more familiar and trustworthy. Brand recognition closes the gap."

**"We're at full patient capacity."**
"That's a wonderful position. Consider this: higher-income families from [COMMUNITY] tend to accept more elective treatments — sealants, cosmetic ortho, Invisalign. They increase your revenue per patient. It's not more patients — it's better patients."

**"Parents research online, not through mail."**
"They do — and that's exactly why brand recognition matters. A study from the DMA found that 60% of people who receive direct mail go online to research the business afterward. Your postcard drives them to your website and Google reviews, where they see your 4.8 stars and book."

## Close

"Can I send you a sample ad layout and the specific [COMMUNITY] routes? If it resonates, we can discuss what offer to highlight — maybe a new patient special or a free ortho consultation."
