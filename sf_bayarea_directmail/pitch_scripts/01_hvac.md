# Pitch Script — HVAC / Heating & Air Conditioning

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. I'm putting together an exclusive advertising campaign for [COMMUNITY] — [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households, median income [MEDIAN HHI] — and I'm looking for the one HVAC company to feature. Is that something you'd want to hear about? It takes about two minutes."

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"The reason I'm calling you specifically is that we only allow one HVAC company per campaign. So whoever gets this spot is the only heating and air company that [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homeowners in [COMMUNITY] will see. No competition on the card."

**The Seasonal Play:**
"HVAC is the perfect direct mail category because every homeowner needs it — it's not if, it's when. And when their system goes down at 2 AM in July or December, they reach for whatever's closest. That postcard on their fridge? That's you."

**The Numbers:**
"Your investment is [STANDARD PRICE] for a standard ad space. That works out to about [COST PER HOME] per household. Compare that to Google Ads — a single click for 'HVAC repair [COMMUNITY]' costs $30–50, and that click might not even convert. Here, you're in front of [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homeowners for one flat fee."

**The Neighborhood Fit:**
"[COMMUNITY] homeowners have an average household income of [MEDIAN HHI] — these are people who maintain their systems, upgrade to high-efficiency units, and call a professional rather than DIY. They're your ideal customer."

## Objection Handling

**"We get all our business from Google/Yelp."**
"That's great — and those are important channels. But here's the thing: Google only works when someone is actively searching. Direct mail puts your name in their home before they even have a problem. When the AC goes out, you want to be the name they already know, not the third result on page one."

**"We tried direct mail before and it didn't work."**
"Was it a solo mailer, or were you one of 20 businesses in a coupon pack? The reason shared postcards work differently is exclusivity — you're the only HVAC company. And we're not blanketing random zip codes — we're targeting [COMMUNITY] specifically because of the income level and homeownership rate."

**"That's too expensive."**
"I understand — let me frame it differently. If your average repair call is $300–500, you only need 2 customers from [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households to break even. And those 2 customers become lifetime clients — annual maintenance, replacements, referrals. The lifetime value of one HVAC customer is $5,000+."

## Close

"Here's what I'd suggest: let me send you a mockup of what your ad space would look like on the postcard, along with the EDDM route map showing exactly which [COMMUNITY] neighborhoods we're covering. Then we can hop on a 10-minute call to finalize. Sound fair?"
