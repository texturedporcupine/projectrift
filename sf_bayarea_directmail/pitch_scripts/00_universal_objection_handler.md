# Universal Objection Handler

These objection responses work across all business categories. They are market-neutral and do not require Bay Area-specific customization.

---

## "We don't do direct mail."

**Response:**
"I completely understand — and honestly, most of the businesses I work with said the same thing before their first campaign. The reason direct mail works differently here is three things: one, you're the ONLY business in your category, so there's no competition on the card. Two, we're targeting a specific affluent neighborhood, not blanketing random zip codes. And three, USPS data shows direct mail has a 90% open rate — no other advertising channel comes close. I'm not asking you to commit to direct mail as a strategy. I'm asking if you'd be open to seeing how one campaign in [COMMUNITY] could work for your business."

---

## "We tried direct mail before and it didn't work."

**Response:**
"Can I ask what you tried? [Listen.] Most businesses that had a bad experience with direct mail were either part of a coupon pack with 30 other businesses, or they did a solo mailer to a random zip code. Both of those have poor ROI. What we do is fundamentally different: a curated postcard with 6–8 non-competing local businesses, targeted to a specific high-income community via USPS EDDM. Category exclusivity means your ad doesn't compete with anyone else on the card. And the shared cost model means your investment is a fraction of what a solo mailer would cost."

---

## "It's too expensive."

**Response:**
"I hear you. Let me reframe the math. Your investment is [PRICE], which reaches [HOUSEHOLDS] households. That's [COST PER HOME] per home. For comparison: a single Google Ads click in your category costs $[GOOGLE CPC] — and that click might not convert. Here, you're physically in [HOUSEHOLDS] homes for one flat fee. Now, what's your average job worth? [Listen.] So if you close just [BREAK-EVEN NUMBER] new customers from [HOUSEHOLDS] households, you've made your investment back. Everything after that is profit. And those new customers become repeat customers and referral sources."

## "I need to think about it."

**Response:**
"Absolutely — take whatever time you need. I don't want to rush you into anything. A couple of things to keep in mind while you're considering it: we only allow one [CATEGORY] provider per campaign, and I am actively reaching out to other businesses in your category. Once that spot is taken, it's locked for this campaign. I'd hate for you to decide yes and find out the spot went to someone else. Can I follow up with you on [SPECIFIC DAY] to see where you've landed?"

## "We're too busy / fully booked."

**Response:**
"That's actually the best time to be doing this kind of marketing. When you're busy, you can be selective — direct mail from [COMMUNITY] brings you higher-value clients from an affluent neighborhood. Better clients, better jobs, better margins. Plus, this isn't about tomorrow — it's about building your pipeline for next month and next quarter. Consistent marketing when you're busy prevents the feast-and-famine cycle."

---

## "We only do word of mouth."

**Response:**
"Word of mouth is the gold standard, and I'd never want to replace it. What direct mail does is amplify it. Here's what happens: your existing customers in [COMMUNITY] see the postcard and feel validated in their choice. Their neighbors see it and recognize your name when they get a referral. That double touchpoint — postcard plus word of mouth — converts at a much higher rate than either one alone."

And our model is specifically designed for high ROI: exclusive category placement in an affluent, high-homeownership community. After this campaign, I'll have real local data to share — and my first-campaign advertisers get the best pricing."

**Response (if repeat campaign):**
"Absolutely. In our last [COMMUNITY/NEARBY] campaign, [ADVERTISER] in the [CATEGORY] category reported [X] calls/leads within the first two weeks. Here's what they said: '[TESTIMONIAL].' I'm happy to connect you with them directly if you'd like a reference."

## "What if I don't get any calls?"

**Response:**
"That's a fair concern. Here's why I'm confident: we're not sending your ad to random people. We're sending it to [HOUSEHOLDS] homeowners in [COMMUNITY] with a median income of [MEDIAN HHI]. These are people who use services like yours. Even at a conservative 0.5% response rate, that's [RESPONSE NUMBER] potential leads. And unlike digital ads, the postcard doesn't disappear — it sits on counters and fridges for days or weeks, generating impressions long after delivery. That said, no advertising is guaranteed. What I can guarantee is that your postcard will physically land in [HOUSEHOLDS] homes, and you'll be the only [CATEGORY] on the card."
