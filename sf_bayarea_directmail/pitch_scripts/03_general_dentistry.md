# Pitch Script — General / Family Dentistry

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. I'm putting together a direct mail campaign reaching [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households in [COMMUNITY] and I'm reserving one spot for a dentist. I thought of your practice because [PERSONALIZED REASON — location, reviews, etc.]. Do you have a quick minute?"

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"What makes this different from any other advertising is that you'd be the only dental practice on the postcard. If [COMMUNITY] families need a new dentist — and they're always looking — yours is the name they see."

**The Family Play:**
"[COMMUNITY] is a family-driven community — household size of 2.5+, strong schools, median income [MEDIAN HHI]. These families need a dentist for every member of the household. One new patient family of four is worth $2,000–3,000/year in ongoing care."

**The Numbers:**
"The investment is [STANDARD PRICE] for a standard ad space. The average new dental patient is worth $600–800 in year one (exams, cleanings, X-rays, treatment). Over a 5-year patient lifetime, that's $3,000–5,000. You need one new patient family to make this campaign wildly profitable."

**New Mover Angle:**
"Here's something a lot of dentists don't think about: one of the first things families do when they move to a new area is find a dentist. [COMMUNITY] has steady turnover in its housing market. Your postcard is sitting in their mailbox during that exact decision window."

## Objection Handling

**"We're already fully booked."**
"That's a great problem — and it actually makes this even better. Direct mail builds brand awareness for the long term. When your patients refer friends, you want those friends to already recognize your name. And premium ad placement positions you as THE dentist of [COMMUNITY]."

**"We rely on insurance referrals."**
"Insurance gets patients in the door, but it's not selective about which door. Direct mail lets you target the exact neighborhood around your practice — patients who live close, have high income, and are likely to accept treatment recommendations. These are your best patients."

**"Our patients come from Google."**
"Google is reactive — someone searches when they have a toothache. Direct mail is proactive — you're in their home before they need you. When they do search, your name is already familiar. Brand recognition increases click-through rates on Google by 2–3x."

## Close

"Can I send you a sample layout showing your practice featured on the postcard, along with the exact [COMMUNITY] routes we're covering? Then if it looks good, we can talk about what offer you'd want to feature — maybe a new patient special or a family package."
