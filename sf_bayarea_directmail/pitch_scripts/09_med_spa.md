# Pitch Script — Med Spa / Aesthetics

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. I'm curating an exclusive advertising campaign for [COMMUNITY] — [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households, median income [MEDIAN HHI] — and I want to feature one med spa or aesthetics practice. Your practice came up as a strong fit. Do you have a quick minute?"

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"One aesthetics provider per card. No competing med spas, no competing dermatology practices. You own the category."

**The Affluent Demo Play:**
"[COMMUNITY] residents have the income and the inclination for aesthetic services — Botox, fillers, laser treatments, body contouring. This isn't mass marketing — it's precision targeting of your ideal demographic: affluent homeowners ages 30–65 who invest in self-care."

**The Numbers:**
"Average first-visit spend at a med spa is $300–500. Returning clients spend $2,000–5,000/year on treatments. One new client from [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households gives you a 3–5x return on [STANDARD PRICE]. And med spa clients are some of the most loyal repeat customers in any service category."

**The Trust Factor:**
"Med spa is an industry where trust matters enormously. Being featured on a curated community postcard — alongside other trusted local businesses — positions your practice as part of the [COMMUNITY] establishment. It's an endorsement by association."

## Objection Handling

**"We use Instagram and Google Ads."**
"Those channels are important for visual discovery. But Instagram's organic reach is under 5%, and Google Ads for 'Botox [CITY]' cost $20–40 per click. Direct mail reaches [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homes at a fraction of the cost, and it doesn't require the homeowner to be scrolling at the right moment."

**"Our clients come through referrals."**
"Referrals are gold — and direct mail supercharges them. When your existing client mentions your practice to a friend, and that friend already received your postcard, you've created a double touchpoint. Recognition + referral = booking."

**"Our services are premium — direct mail feels downmarket."**
"That's why we design a premium postcard, not a coupon pack. This is a curated selection of 6–8 quality local businesses on a professionally designed 8.5x11 card delivered via USPS. Think of it as a community concierge card. Your premium practice deserves premium presentation, and that's exactly what we deliver."

## Close

"I'd love to show you a sample layout. For med spas, we tend to use the premium ad placement — larger space, front or back feature — which at [PREMIUM PRICE] gives you the visual impact your brand deserves. Can I send a mockup over?"
