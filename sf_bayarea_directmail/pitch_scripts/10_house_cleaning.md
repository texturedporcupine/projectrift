# Pitch Script — House Cleaning / Maid Service

## Opening Hook

"Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME] with Bay Area Direct Mail. We're putting together an exclusive advertising campaign targeting [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] homeowners in [COMMUNITY] — and I need one house cleaning company. You'd be the only cleaning service on the card. Is that something you'd be interested in?"

## Key Talking Points

**The Exclusivity Angle:**
"One cleaning company per campaign. When [COMMUNITY] homeowners decide they need cleaning help — and with dual-income households at [MEDIAN HHI], many of them do — you're the name they see."

**The Dual-Income Play:**
"[COMMUNITY] is full of dual-income professional households. Both partners work. Nobody wants to spend their weekend cleaning. These families actively seek reliable, trustworthy cleaning services — and they're willing to pay market rates for quality."

**The Numbers:**
"A biweekly cleaning client at $200–300/visit is worth $5,000–7,500/year. One new recurring client from [CAMPAIGN HOUSEHOLDS] households and your [STANDARD PRICE] investment returns 7–10x in the first year alone. And cleaning clients who find someone they trust almost never leave."

**The Trust Factor:**
"House cleaning is intimate — you're letting someone into your home. Being featured on a community postcard alongside the neighborhood dentist, HVAC company, and other trusted providers gives you an implicit endorsement. It says 'this business belongs here.'"

## Objection Handling

**"We get all our clients from Yelp/Google."**
"Those platforms work — but they commoditize your service. On Google, you're competing on price with every other cleaner in the metro. On this postcard, you're the only option in your category, positioned next to established [COMMUNITY] businesses. It's a different kind of lead."

**"Our schedule is full."**
"Perfect — that means you can be selective. Use this campaign to fill specific geographic gaps in your schedule. [COMMUNITY] clients clustered in the same neighborhood mean less driving time between jobs and higher daily revenue."

**"We do commercial, not residential."**
"Would you consider adding residential in [COMMUNITY]? Residential cleaning for affluent homeowners is higher-margin per hour than most commercial accounts, and the clients are more consistent. But if it's not your model, I understand."

**"We rely on word of mouth."**
"Word of mouth is slow but powerful. Direct mail is fast and wide. Together they're unstoppable. Your existing [COMMUNITY] clients see the card and feel validated. Their neighbors see it and call you. That's how you build neighborhood density."

## Close

"Can I show you the route map and a sample ad? For cleaning companies, I'd recommend including a 'first cleaning' offer or a free estimate to drive that initial call. We can design around whatever promotion works best for your business."
