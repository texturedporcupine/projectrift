# Bay Area Advertiser Outreach Sequence

Use this sequence for one advertiser per category per community. Replace bracketed
fields before sending.

## Email 1: Cold opener

**Subject:** Exclusive [category] spot for [community] households

Hi [first name],

I am putting together a shared postcard campaign for [community] homeowners and
wanted to offer [business name] first look at the exclusive [category] position.

The card goes to roughly [household count] local households through USPS Every
Door Direct Mail. Each card includes only one business per category, so there
will not be another [category] company next to you.

For [community], the standard ad space is [price], and it includes:

- Placement on a professionally designed oversized postcard
- Category exclusivity for this campaign
- Delivery to selected [community] EDDM routes
- Proof approval before anything prints

Would you be open to a 10-minute call this week to see whether this route set is
a fit for [business name]?

Best,

[sender name]

## Email 2: Day-3 follow-up

**Subject:** Re: [community] direct mail spot

Hi [first name],

Quick follow-up on the [community] postcard campaign.

The reason I thought of [business name] is that homeowners in [community] tend
to respond well to local, trusted service providers, especially when the offer is
timed around [seasonal hook: spring HVAC tune-ups / pool openings / back-to-school
tutoring / year-end dental benefits].

The campaign is designed to be simple:

1. Pick one offer or reason to call.
2. Approve the ad proof.
3. We mail to the selected routes.
4. You track calls, QR scans, or offer mentions.

If you are interested, I can send the route map and pricing for the [category]
slot.

Best,

[sender name]

## Email 3: Day-7 value-add

**Subject:** Break-even math for the [community] card

Hi [first name],

One last note in case it helps evaluate the [community] postcard.

For most local service categories, the break-even math is modest:

- Standard placement: [price]
- Estimated delivery: [household count] households
- Needed response to break even: often 1-3 booked jobs, depending on your
  average ticket

Examples:

- HVAC: one replacement estimate can justify the campaign.
- Dentistry: a few new-patient appointments can cover the slot.
- Pool or landscaping: one recurring account can pay for the mailer.
- Med spa: a small number of booked consultations can cover the campaign.

I am only placing one [category] business on the postcard. If you want to review
the route map, I can send it over.

Best,

[sender name]

## Phone follow-up opener

Hi [first name], this is [sender name]. I sent a note about the shared postcard
campaign for [community] homeowners. I am holding one exclusive [category] spot
and wanted to see if it is worth sending you the route map and pricing.

## In-person visit opener

I am organizing a local homeowner mailer for [community]. It is a shared
postcard, but every category is exclusive. I am speaking with one [category]
business before offering the spot elsewhere. Who is the right person to review a
small local advertising opportunity?
