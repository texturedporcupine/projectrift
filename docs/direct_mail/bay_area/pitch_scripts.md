# Bay Area Category Pitch Scripts

Use these as call or in-person visit scripts. Replace bracketed fields before
sending written material. Keep category exclusivity as the main differentiator.

## Shared opening

Hi, this is [Your Name]. I am putting together a shared postcard for
[Community] households. It is a category-exclusive mailer: one HVAC company, one
dentist, one pest-control company, and so on. I am reaching out because your
business looks like a strong local fit, and I want to see whether you would like
to lock the [Category] spot before I offer it elsewhere.

The card goes to roughly [Route Count] high-income households through USPS EDDM.
You get professional design, a clear offer, and category exclusivity without
having to buy an entire solo mail campaign.

## HVAC

### Hook

Danville and San Ramon homeowners are ideal HVAC customers: larger homes,
high comfort expectations, and strong replacement economics before summer heat
or winter service calls.

### Pitch

Your break-even is small. If a `$650` standard slot generates one tune-up that
turns into a repair or one replacement estimate, the campaign can pay for itself.
The postcard puts your brand in the home before the urgent no-AC call happens.

### Offer ideas

- `$79` seasonal tune-up for [Community] residents.
- Free second opinion on replacement quotes.
- Priority scheduling for homes in `[ZIP]`.

### Close

I am holding one HVAC slot for this card. If you want it, the deposit reserves
the category and keeps competitors off the campaign.

## Universal objection handler

### "We do not do direct mail."

That makes sense. Most solo direct mail is expensive and broad. This is narrower:
you are sharing the cost with non-competing businesses, and you get category
exclusivity in a defined route cluster. The question is whether one or two
responses from these households would justify the slot.

### "We tried direct mail before."

Totally fair. What was different about the prior campaign: solo mailer or shared
card, which neighborhoods, and did you have a dedicated offer or tracking number?
This campaign is designed to be measured by route, offer, and category
exclusivity.

### "It is too expensive."

I understand. The standard slot is designed to be below the cost of a small solo
mailer. For most categories, the break-even is one booked job, one new patient,
or a small number of recurring customers. If that math does not work for your
average ticket, it is probably not the right fit.

### "I need to think about it."

Of course. The only timing issue is category exclusivity. I can hold the
[Category] slot until [Date]. If you want it reserved, the 50% deposit locks it
in and I will not pitch another [Category] business for this card.
