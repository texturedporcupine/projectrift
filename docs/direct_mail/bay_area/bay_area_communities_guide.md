# Bay Area Communities Guide

Use this guide to choose the first shared-postcard campaign market and to
customize pitch materials for local advertisers. The best targets have clear
geographic boundaries, high homeownership, strong school/community identity, and
commercial corridors filled with owner-operated service businesses.

## Community comparison

| Community | Zip codes | Median HHI | Home count | Tier | Recommended categories |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Danville | 94526 | $185K+ | 18,000+ | 1 | HVAC, landscaping, pool, dentist, med spa |
| San Ramon | 94582, 94583 | $160K+ | 30,000+ | 1-2 | HVAC, tutoring, family dentist, pest control |
| Pleasanton | 94566, 94588 | $155K+ | 28,000+ | 2 | Pool, landscaping, orthodontics, cleaning |

| Los Gatos | 95030, 95032 | $200K+ | 12,000+ | 1 | Med spa, luxury home services, pool, dentist |

## Selection criteria

1. Confirm 2-3 EDDM carrier routes totaling 5,000-7,000 households.
2. Prioritize household income above $100K and household size above 2.5 where
   USPS EDDM filters support it.
3. Check for active HOA, neighborhood association, school foundation, local
   newsletter, or local paper presence.
4. Map nearby commercial corridors before outreach.
5. Build 30-40 prospects across 8-10 non-competing categories.

## Danville, CA 94526

**Tier:** 1  
**Positioning:** Premium East Bay family community with a strong downtown, high
homeownership, and a mail-reading culture similar to upscale master-planned
suburbs.

### Why it works

- Strong local identity around downtown Danville, schools, parks, and family
  activities.
- High household income supports premium home services, health, dental, and
  aesthetic offers.
- Local businesses benefit from category exclusivity because residents often
  choose familiar providers for repeat household needs.

### Suggested EDDM route approach

- Start with 94526 and identify routes around established residential pockets
  near downtown Danville, Greenbrook, Diablo Road, and Camino Tassajara.
- Favor routes with dense single-family homes and household counts that combine
  to 5,000-7,000 pieces.
- Confirm the servicing Destination Delivery Unit before quoting drop-off
  logistics.

### Best advertiser categories

| Priority | Category | Pitch angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | HVAC | Pre-season tune-up for high-value homes before summer or winter demand spikes |
| 2 | Landscaping | Premium curb appeal and recurring maintenance for family neighborhoods |
| 3 | Pool service | Weekly service and repair for homes with pools in established areas |
| 4 | General dentistry | Family appointment availability and local trust |
| 5 | Med spa/aesthetics | Premium self-care offer for high-income households |
| 6 | Pest control | Seasonal prevention for suburban homes |
| 7 | Roofing | Inspection and repair before rainy season |
| 8 | House cleaning | Recurring cleaning for busy professional families |

### Prospecting notes

Lead with exclusivity: "only one HVAC company" or "only one dental office" on
the Danville card. Local proof matters; references to downtown Danville, school
families, and established homeowners will land better than generic Bay Area
language.

## San Ramon, CA 94582 / 94583

**Tier:** 1-2  
**Positioning:** Large, affluent, master-planned East Bay market with tech
families, newer housing stock, and strong school/community identity.

### Why it works

- Large household base allows multiple future route clusters after a successful
  first drop.
- Bishop Ranch and surrounding commercial corridors create a dense advertiser
  pool.
- Tech and professional families respond well to clear, offer-driven household
  services and children's enrichment.

### Suggested EDDM route approach

- Evaluate 94582 and 94583 separately, then pick the route cluster with the best
  mix of household income, household size, and route density.
- Consider neighborhoods near Windemere, Dougherty Valley, Gale Ranch, and
  established residential pockets closer to central San Ramon.
- Avoid over-broad geography on the first campaign; a tight route cluster is
  easier to sell and easier for advertisers to understand.

### Best advertiser categories

| Priority | Category | Pitch angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tutoring/enrichment | Reach school-focused households with a local academic offer |
| 2 | HVAC | Seasonal tune-up or replacement quote for newer family homes |
| 3 | Family dentistry | Family scheduling and new patient offers |
| 4 | Pest control | Preventive home treatment for suburban neighborhoods |
| 5 | House cleaning | Recurring cleanings for busy professional families |
| 6 | Landscaping | Maintenance and yard upgrades for HOA-visible homes |
| 7 | Orthodontics | Parent-driven demand among school-age families |
| 8 | Plumbing | Trusted emergency and maintenance provider |

### Prospecting notes

For family-focused categories, connect the campaign to school-year timing,
summer planning, and household convenience. For home services, emphasize route
density and repeated brand exposure in a defined neighborhood.

## Pleasanton, CA 94566 / 94588

**Tier:** 2  
**Positioning:** Established, affluent Tri-Valley suburb with strong schools,
family households, and a mix of downtown, residential, and commuter-driven
business activity.

### Why it works

- Large addressable base across two zip codes.
- Strong homeownership and neighborhood pride support home improvement,
  cleaning, dental, and family-service offers.
- Advertisers can understand the value of reaching a defined Pleasanton route
  cluster rather than buying general Bay Area impressions.

### Suggested EDDM route approach

- Start with 94566 for older established neighborhoods or 94588 for larger
  suburban clusters.
- Compare routes near Ruby Hill, Vintage Hills, Val Vista, and residential areas
  with strong household counts.
- Keep the first drop to a coherent section of town so advertiser reporting is
  simple.

### Best advertiser categories

| Priority | Category | Pitch angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pool service | Recurring pool care and repair for established homeowners |
| 2 | Landscaping | Maintenance, irrigation, and curb appeal |
| 3 | Orthodontics | School-age family demand and local practice differentiation |
| 4 | House cleaning | Recurring service for dual-income households |
| 5 | HVAC | Seasonal service and replacement quotes |
| 6 | Pest control | Preventive household service |
| 7 | Plumbing | Trusted local emergency provider |
| 8 | Pediatric dentistry | New patient acquisition for family households |

### Prospecting notes

Pleasanton is broad enough that advertisers may ask "which part?" Be ready with
route screenshots, route counts, and a one-sentence description of the exact
neighborhood cluster.

## Dublin, CA 94568

**Tier:** 2  
**Positioning:** Fast-growing Tri-Valley community with young families, newer
housing, and strong demand for household services and children's enrichment.

### Why it works

- Growth market with many households still forming local provider preferences.
- Newer homes still need HVAC, pest control, cleaning, landscaping, and family
  services.
- Strong family demographics create opportunities for tutoring, dentistry,
  orthodontics, and youth-focused services.

### Suggested EDDM route approach

- Evaluate east Dublin route clusters around newer residential development.
- Favor dense single-family or townhome-heavy routes where household counts
  assemble efficiently.
- Confirm whether a smaller first campaign should target 4,000-5,500 pieces or
  whether route density supports the full 5,000-7,000 household goal.

### Best advertiser categories

| Priority | Category | Pitch angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tutoring/enrichment | Newer family households and school-focused parents |
| 2 | HVAC | Preventive maintenance for newer homes |
| 3 | Pest control | Seasonal protection and new-home pest prevention |
| 4 | Family dentistry | New resident and family appointment offers |
| 5 | House cleaning | Busy families and commuting professionals |
| 6 | Landscaping | Yard completion, upgrades, and maintenance |
| 7 | Plumbing | Maintenance relationship for growing households |
| 8 | Pediatric dentistry | Local practice discovery among young families |

### Prospecting notes

Newer businesses and newer residents make Dublin a good second or third
campaign market. Use language around "becoming the familiar local provider" in
fast-growing neighborhoods.

## Los Gatos, CA 95030 / 95032

**Tier:** 1  
**Positioning:** Premium South Bay market with high household income, strong
local identity, and strong demand for luxury home services, wellness, dental,
and aesthetic categories.

### Why it works

- Very high income and strong home values support premium-priced advertiser
  categories.
- Well-defined local identity makes a Los Gatos card more compelling than a
  generic South Bay campaign.
- Local business corridors include many premium service providers that can
  justify higher ad pricing.

### Suggested EDDM route approach

- Compare 95030 and 95032 routes by income, household count, and route
  compactness.
- Favor residential clusters with premium single-family homes and practical
  DDU drop-off logistics.
- Keep the first card selective; Los Gatos supports Tier 1 pricing when route
  quality is clear.

### Best advertiser categories

| Priority | Category | Pitch angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Med spa/aesthetics | Premium self-care and appointment-driven offers |
| 2 | Luxury home services | Remodeling, design, roofing, estate maintenance |
| 3 | Landscaping | Premium outdoor living and curb appeal |
| 4 | Pool service | High-value recurring service and repair |
| 5 | General dentistry | High-trust local provider and family appointments |
| 6 | Estate planning | Local professional services for high-asset households |
| 7 | Pest control | Discreet preventive home care |
| 8 | House cleaning | Premium recurring home care |

### Prospecting notes

Lead with quality, not cheap reach. Advertisers should see the campaign as a
premium neighborhood placement with category exclusivity, not a commodity print
buy.
