households that are likely to hire premium home, family, and wellness providers.

## Slide 3: How the shared postcard works

**Headline:** A local category-exclusive campaign without owning the logistics.

1. We select 2-3 USPS EDDM routes in the target community.
2. Each advertiser receives one exclusive category slot.
3. We coordinate creative, proofs, print production, EDDM bundling, and DDU
   drop-off.
4. The postcard lands in every selected household mailbox during the same local
   campaign window.

**Exclusivity examples:**

- One HVAC company.
- One dentist.
- One pest control provider.
- One pool service company.
- One med spa or aesthetics provider.
