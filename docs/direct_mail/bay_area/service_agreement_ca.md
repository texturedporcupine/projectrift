# Shared Postcard Advertising Agreement - California Template

> Template only. Have a California attorney review before use.

This Shared Postcard Advertising Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
`[Effective Date]` by and between `[Operator Legal Name]`, doing business as
`[Fictitious Business Name if applicable]` ("Publisher"), and
`[Advertiser Legal Name]` ("Advertiser").

## 1. Campaign

Publisher will coordinate one shared postcard advertising campaign for:

- Community: `[Danville / San Ramon / Pleasanton / Dublin / Los Gatos]`
- Target ZIP code(s): `[ZIP codes]`
- Estimated mailed households: `[5,000-8,000]`
- Postcard size: `[8.5 x 11 / 6.5 x 9]`
- Category exclusivity: `[Advertiser category]`
- Placement: `[Standard / Premium]`

Publisher will coordinate design, advertiser proof review, print production,
USPS Every Door Direct Mail paperwork, and DDU drop-off logistics.

## 2. Category Exclusivity

Publisher will not sell another ad space on the same postcard campaign to a
direct competitor in Advertiser's locked category. Category exclusivity applies
only to the campaign listed in this Agreement and does not apply to future
campaigns unless renewed in writing.

## 3. Advertiser Responsibilities

Advertiser will provide:

- Logo files, brand colors, and preferred contact information.
- Offer or call to action.
- Any required professional license numbers or required disclosures.
- Proof approval or requested revisions by the stated deadline.

Advertiser is responsible for the accuracy and legality of claims, offers,
licensing statements, testimonials, and disclosures in its advertisement.

## 4. Fees and Payment

Total campaign fee: `$[amount]`.

Payment schedule:

1. 50% deposit due at signing to reserve the category.
2. Remaining 50% due before printing.

Publisher may pause design, printing, or mailing until required payments are
received. Deposits are non-refundable once design work begins or a category is
held off market.

## 5. Proof Approval

Publisher will provide a digital proof before printing. Advertiser must approve
the proof in writing. Publisher is not responsible for errors that were present
in a proof approved by Advertiser.

## 6. Mailing and Delivery

Publisher will use USPS Every Door Direct Mail for delivery to selected carrier
routes. Delivery dates are estimates and may be affected by USPS operations,
weather, holidays, or other conditions outside Publisher's control.

## 7. Cancellation

If Advertiser cancels before proof approval, Advertiser remains responsible for
work completed and any non-refundable production costs incurred. If Advertiser
cancels after proof approval or after print production begins, the full campaign
fee remains due.

## 8. Compliance

Advertiser represents that its ad content complies with applicable federal,
California, county, city, and industry-specific advertising rules. Publisher may
reject or request changes to content that appears misleading, unlawful, or
inconsistent with campaign standards.

## 9. California Fictitious Business Name

If Publisher operates under a fictitious business name, Publisher will maintain
any required California Fictitious Business Name filing, publication, and renewal
records in the county where required.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

Publisher does not guarantee a specific number of leads, calls, customers, or
sales. Publisher's liability under this Agreement is limited to the amount paid
by Advertiser for the campaign.

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. Venue for any
dispute will be in the California county where Publisher's principal business
office is located, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.
