# Cursor Context: SF Bay Area Shared Postcard Direct Mail

## Project summary

This is a direct mail advertising business for affluent Bay Area suburban
communities. The operator sells exclusive ad slots to non-competing local
businesses on one shared postcard, coordinates design and printing, and delivers
to 5,000-8,000 households using USPS Every Door Direct Mail (EDDM).

The model is adapted from an Austin, Texas playbook. The core workflow remains
the same, but legal, tax, pricing, geography, and local-market references should
use California and Bay Area assumptions.

## Core offer

- One shared postcard per campaign.
- One advertiser per business category per postcard.
- 6-8 advertisers per card.
- 8.5" x 11" or 6.5" x 9" postcard format.
- 14pt cardstock or better.
- USPS EDDM delivery to selected carrier routes.
- 50% deposit at signing and 50% final payment before printing.
- Operator handles creative coordination, proof approvals, printing, bundling,
  EDDM paperwork, DDU drop-off, and advertiser follow-up.

## California legal notes

- Sole proprietorship remains the simplest launch structure.
- File a Fictitious Business Name (FBN) statement with the county recorder.
- Publish the FBN in an adjudicated local newspaper for 4 consecutive weeks
  within 30 days of filing.
- Keep the affidavit of publication on file.
- Most cities/counties require a local business license; budget $50-200/year.
- California LLCs carry an $800/year minimum franchise tax through the Franchise
  Tax Board.
- Advertising services are generally not taxable, but printed materials sold to
  clients may be taxable. Confirm treatment with CDTFA guidance or a California
  tax professional.

## EDDM operating requirements

- Use eddm.usps.com to select carrier routes.
- Start by entering Danville 94526 or San Ramon 94582.
- Filter for household income $100K+ and household size 2.5+ where available.
- Select 2-3 routes totaling 5,000-7,000 households for the first campaign.
- Prepare USPS EDDM paperwork and route bundle slips.
- Drop at the Destination Delivery Unit serving the selected routes.
- Pay $0.247 per piece at drop-off unless USPS pricing changes.

## Writing style

- Specific to Bay Area suburbs, but not overly broad "Bay Area" language.
- Emphasize neighborhood exclusivity, category ownership, affluent homeowners,
  and trackable local response.
- Avoid promising results. Use "designed to reach", "intended to generate", and
  "track response" rather than guarantees.
- Keep outreach concise and practical. Lead with the exclusive category slot.
