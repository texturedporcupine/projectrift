## 5. Printing and Delivery

**5.1 Print Specifications.** Postcard will be printed on 14pt cardstock (minimum 0.007" thick), meeting USPS EDDM flat-mail dimensional requirements.

**5.2 EDDM Delivery.** Publisher will prepare USPS EDDM bundles and deliver to the Destination Delivery Unit (DDU) serving the target zip code(s). Advertiser acknowledges that EDDM delivers to all households on selected carrier routes and that Publisher does not control the exact delivery date within the USPS system.

**5.3 Delivery Confirmation.** Publisher will provide USPS EDDM receipt(s) confirming postage payment and route selection within 5 business days of drop-off.

**5.4 Household Count.** Estimated household count is based on USPS EDDM route data at time of planning and may vary slightly from final count at DDU drop-off. Publisher is not responsible for USPS count variances of less than 10%.

## 6. Cancellation

**6.1 Cancellation by Advertiser.** If Advertiser cancels:
- Before artwork deadline: Advertiser forfeits deposit
- After artwork approved / proof signed off: Advertiser owes 100% of Total Fee
- After print order placed: Advertiser owes 100% of Total Fee and may not receive a refund

**6.2 Cancellation by Publisher.** If Publisher cancels the campaign due to insufficient advertiser fill rate (fewer than 4 advertisers confirmed):
- Publisher will notify Advertiser within 5 business days
- Publisher will refund 100% of any amounts paid within 10 business days
- Publisher's liability is limited to amounts paid; no consequential damages

**6.3 Publisher Cancellation for Other Reasons.** If Publisher cancels for reasons other than insufficient fill rate, Publisher will refund 100% of amounts paid and use reasonable efforts to offer Advertiser priority placement in the next equivalent campaign.
