# Bay Area Shared Postcard Direct Mail — Community Profiles

*Reference guide for selecting EDDM target communities and building prospect lists.*

---

## How to Use This Guide

For each campaign, pick one community. Lock 2-3 EDDM carrier routes at eddm.usps.com (filter: HHI $100K+, household size 2.5+). Build a 30-40 business prospect list across 8-10 non-competing categories before any outreach. This guide gives you the community context to do that efficiently.

## Community 1 — Danville, CA

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Zip codes** | 94526 |
| **Tier** | Tier 2 – Strong ($550-750 standard / $850-1,100 premium) |
| **Median HHI** | $185,000+ |
| **Total households** | ~18,000 |
| **Recommended EDDM target** | 5,000-7,000 households (2-3 routes in Blackhawk, Sycamore Valley, or downtown Danville area) |
| **Homeownership rate** | ~75% |

### Community Character

Danville is an affluent, community-oriented suburb in the San Ramon Valley with a strong small-town identity. The downtown area anchored by Hartz Avenue has a loyal local business ecosystem. Blackhawk is an HOA-governed master-planned community with its own newsletter and active residents' association — residents read and respond to community mail. The Crow Canyon Country Club and Blackhawk Country Club draw high-net-worth households that are the ideal demographic for premium home services and aesthetics. Community identity here is analogous to Circle C Ranch in Austin: tight-knit, mail-reading culture, high trust in local recommendations.

### Why Danville Works

- Active HOA infrastructure across Blackhawk, Alamo Creek, Sycamore Valley subdivisions
- Local newspaper (Danville Weekly / Pleasanton Weekly) makes FBN publication easy and cheap
- Dense local commercial corridor on Camino Tassajara and Sycamore Valley Road
- High homeownership means residents have homes worth servicing (HVAC, pool, landscaping)
- Premium demographic supports med spa, dentistry, and concierge services

### Recommended Ad Categories

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| HVAC | Peak demand spring/fall; Blackhawk homes are large with multi-zone systems |
| Landscaping | Mandatory HOA curb appeal; drought-tolerant redesigns are a strong hook |
| Pool service | Significant pool ownership in Blackhawk and Sycamore Valley |
| General dentistry | Family-oriented community; cosmetic dentistry also viable |
| Med spa / aesthetics | Strong HHI supports Botox, filler, laser — book off Hartz Ave businesses |
| Pest control | Warm inland climate; ants and rodents are perennial concerns |
| House cleaning | Dual-income tech households; recurring revenue pitch |
| Pediatric dentistry | Family demographic with school-age children |

### Prospect Corridor

Commercial corridors serving Danville: Hartz Avenue (downtown), Camino Tassajara, Sycamore Valley Road, Diablo Road, San Ramon Valley Blvd at the Danville/San Ramon line.

### First Campaign Recommendation

Routes: Blackhawk area (94506 adjacent) + southern Danville (Sycamore Valley). Target 5,500 households. 6 advertisers at $600 standard = $3,600 gross. Costs: ~$350 print + ~$1,359 EDDM = ~$1,709. **Estimated net: ~$1,900.**

## Community 2 — San Ramon, CA

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Zip codes** | 94582, 94583 |
| **Tier** | Tier 2 – Strong ($550-750 standard / $850-1,100 premium) |
| **Median HHI** | $160,000+ |
| **Total households** | ~30,000 |
| **Recommended EDDM target** | 5,000-7,000 households (Bishop Ranch corridor, Dougherty Valley, or Gale Ranch) |
| **Homeownership rate** | ~70% |

### Community Character

San Ramon is a planned business-and-residential community anchored by Bishop Ranch, one of the largest office parks in California, home to Chevron, AT&T, and dozens of tech companies. The residential population is heavily tech-professional and dual-income. Gale Ranch (94582) is the premier master-planned subdivision with multiple HOAs, a strong community newsletter, and very high homeownership among families with children. Dougherty Valley is newer and dense with young families — strong tutoring and pediatric dentistry demand.

### Why San Ramon Works

- Multiple distinct HOA-governed subdivisions with community newsletters
- Bishop Ranch proximity = business owners who understand ROI and marketing
- Tech-professional households: high disposable income, busy schedules = premium services
- Very high school enrollment = pediatric, tutoring demand
- Consistent mail engagement in HOA communities

### Recommended Ad Categories

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| HVAC | Large newer homes; maintenance plans are a recurring revenue pitch |
| Tutoring | Highest-priority category — intense academic culture, Khan Lab School proximity |
| Family / general dentistry | Dense family demographic; pediatric add-on viable |
| Pest control | Warm summers; valley grasshoppers and ants are seasonal hooks |
| House cleaning | Dual-income tech households; emphasize recurring service |
| Landscaping | HOA curb appeal requirements; drought-compliant redesign hook |
| Roofing | Newer construction; Class A fire-rated materials pitch resonates |
| Med spa | Professional population; lunchtime treatment hook (near Bishop Ranch) |

### Prospect Corridor

Commercial areas: Bollinger Canyon Road, Crow Canyon Road, Marketplace at San Ramon (Bishop Ranch 3), Norris Canyon Road retail, Alcosta Boulevard.

### First Campaign Recommendation

Routes: Gale Ranch / Bent Creek area (94582). Target 5,000-6,000 households. 6-7 advertisers at $600-650 = $3,600-4,550 gross. Costs: ~$350 print + ~$1,236-1,482 EDDM = ~$1,586-1,832. **Estimated net: ~$2,000-2,700.**

### Why Pleasanton Works

- Named one of the "Best Places to Live" multiple times (CNN Money) — residents are proud and engaged
- Ruby Hill HOA = captive premium audience

## Community 4 — Dublin, CA

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Zip codes** | 94568 |
| **Tier** | Tier 3 – Growth ($400-600 standard / $650-850 premium) |
| **Median HHI** | $145,000+ |
| **Total households** | ~22,000 |
| **Recommended EDDM target** | 5,000-7,000 households (Fallon Village, Jordan Ranch, or Schaefer Ranch) |
| **Homeownership rate** | ~65% |

### Community Character

Dublin is one of the fastest-growing cities in California, driven by new master-planned communities like Fallon Village and Jordan Ranch. The population is young, family-oriented, and heavily South Asian and East Asian — creating strong demand for tutoring, family dentistry, and HVAC in new construction. New homes mean first-time buyers who need every service at once and haven't yet built vendor loyalty. This is a Tier 3 market on HHI but the sheer volume of new construction and household formation makes it an excellent growth market.

### Why Dublin Works

- New construction = unestablished vendor relationships (high switching opportunity)
- Large South Asian and East Asian demographic = intensive tutoring and education demand
- Jordan Ranch and Fallon Village have active HOAs and community Facebook groups
- BART access at Dublin/Pleasanton station = commuter demographic receptive to time-saving services
- Lower ad rates = easier first-close for advertisers testing direct mail

### Recommended Ad Categories

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| HVAC | New construction with 1-year builder warranties expiring; maintenance plan pitch |
| Pest control | New development adjacent to open space; rodents and gophers |
| Tutoring | Premier category — intense academic culture, test prep demand |
| Family / general dentistry | Young families; first dentist is a high-value acquisition |
| House cleaning | Dual-income households; new home pride |
| Landscaping | New homes with blank-slate yards; drought-compliant design |
| Roofing | New construction inspection and Class A fire-rated materials |
| Pediatric dentistry | Very high concentration of families with young children |

### Prospect Corridor

Commercial areas: Fallon Road retail, Dublin Blvd corridor, Tassajara Road, Hacienda Drive at I-580.

## Community 5 — Los Gatos, CA

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Zip codes** | 95030, 95032 |
| **Tier** | Tier 1 – Premium ($700-900 standard / $1,100-1,400 premium) |
| **Median HHI** | $200,000+ |
| **Total households** | ~12,000 |
| **Recommended EDDM target** | 4,000-6,000 households (Los Gatos hills, Almaden Valley boundary, or Monte Sereno) |
| **Homeownership rate** | ~80% |

### Community Character

Los Gatos is a premium Silicon Valley suburb with a beautifully preserved downtown, strong local identity, and very high HHI. It sits at the foothills of the Santa Cruz Mountains with large estate-style properties. Residents are tech executives, investors, and established professionals who value quality and discretion. This is the highest-yield community in the guide: 6 advertisers at $800 = $4,800 gross. The downtown has independent boutiques, restaurants, and professional offices — business owners here understand premium marketing.

### Why Los Gatos Works

- Highest HHI of any community profiled — advertisers can charge premium rates and justify it
- Strong community identity with its own newspaper (Los Gatos Weekly-Times) for FBN publication
- HOAs in hillside communities (Monte Sereno, Rinconada Hills) = engaged mail readers
- Low renter rate means most residents have long tenure and established vendor relationships — but exclusivity is the hook: get the one HVAC company before a competitor does
- Netflix, Apple, and Google employees live here — they understand ROI

### Recommended Ad Categories

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Med spa / aesthetics | Premier category — Botox, laser, filler; downtown spa corridor |
| Luxury home renovation | Kitchen/bath remodel; estate additions; very large homes |
| Pool service | Hillside properties with custom pools; white-glove maintenance |
| General dentistry | Premium cosmetic dentistry hook; sedation dentistry option |
| Landscaping | Estate-scale properties; California drought-compliant design |
| House cleaning | Housekeeping / estate management positioning; weekly service |
| HVAC | Custom multi-zone systems; air quality and filtration pitch |
| Concierge / integrative medicine | Premium demographic; concierge primary care or functional medicine |

### Prospect Corridor

Commercial areas: Santa Cruz Avenue (downtown Los Gatos), Los Gatos Blvd, Blossom Hill Road, University Avenue.

### First Campaign Recommendation

Routes: Hillside Los Gatos + Monte Sereno (95030). Target 4,500-5,000 households. 6 advertisers at $800 = $4,800 gross. Costs: ~$350 print + ~$1,112-1,235 EDDM = ~$1,462-1,585. **Estimated net: ~$3,200-3,300.**

## Community Research Checklist (run before each new community)

- [ ] Go to eddm.usps.com → enter zip → filter HHI $100K+, household size 2.5+ → screenshot 2-3 viable routes and note household counts
- [ ] Search "[community] HOA" → confirm active HOA with newsletter
- [ ] Search "[community] community newsletter" → Nextdoor presence, local paper, HOA newsletter
- [ ] Google Maps → identify commercial corridors serving the neighborhood (that's where your advertisers are)
- [ ] Build 30-40 business prospect list across 8-10 non-competing categories before outreach
- [ ] Identify adjudicated local newspaper for FBN publication (required within 30 days of filing)
