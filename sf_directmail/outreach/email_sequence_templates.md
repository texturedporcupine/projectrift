# 3-Email Outreach Sequence — Bay Area Shared Postcard Direct Mail

*Three-email sequence: cold opener → day-3 follow-up → day-7 value-add.*
*Fill in [BRACKETS] with business-specific information before sending.*
*File naming convention: `outreach_[business-name]_email[1-3].md`*

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Identify your target business and the community you're running in.
2. Choose the script for their category (examples below) or use the Universal version.
3. Fill in all [BRACKETS].
4. Send Email 1 → wait 3 business days → send Email 2 → wait 4 business days → send Email 3.
5. After Email 3, call or visit in person. The email sequence warms them; the close happens live.
