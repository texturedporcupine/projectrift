# Pitch script — House Cleaning

**Best fit communities:** Tier 1-2. San Ramon, Pleasanton, Danville, Dublin —
dual-income / busy-parent demographics. Especially strong in Dougherty Valley
(94582) and Ruby Hill (94566).

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}}. I'm putting together a shared
> postcard for {{community}} that goes to about {{hh_per_drop}} households
> around {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I've reserved the **house cleaning** slot
> on the card and wanted to give {{business_name}} first look."

## Hook

> "House cleaning on EDDM in {{community}} works because **the lifetime
> value of a recurring weekly or biweekly customer is enormous** — and the
> postcard hits dual-income households at the moment they're seriously
> considering hiring help. {{anchor_neighborhood}} alone has thousands
> of households in that 'we keep saying we should' state."

## The offer

> "Shared postcard — **one house cleaning company on it, that's you**.
> Goes to about {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} households. Slot is
> **{{slot_price}}**, all-in. Standard play is a 'first deep clean
> $X off' offer that converts to recurring biweekly within the first
> visit."

## Pre-objection (the "we already have a waiting list" preempt)

> "If you're at capacity, this campaign is a tool to **upgrade your book**.
> A {{community}} customer who pays full rate for biweekly is worth a lot
> more than a customer four cities away on a discounted rate, and the
> postcard skews high-end addresses by definition."

## Ask

> "Want me to email the one-pager and the {{community}} delivery footprint,
> or would 10 minutes on a call this week be easier?"

---

## ROI math

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × ~0.4% response = ~24 inquiries
- ~30% close to recurring biweekly = 7 new accounts
- $200/visit × 26 visits/yr × 7 accounts = $36,400 first-year recurring
- 70% renew = $25,000+ in years 2+
- Slot cost {{slot_price}} → 50-70x first-year recurring ROI

## Common objections

- **"We don't take new clients"** — refer overflow out / waitlist; the
  campaign builds your *premium* book.
- **"We're not insured for new zips yet"** — many cleaning shops say this;
  it's actually a real check, encourage them to call insurance before
  signing.
