# Pitch script — Pediatric Dentistry

**Best fit communities:** San Ramon (Dougherty Valley), Dublin Ranch, south
Palo Alto, Fremont (94539), Pleasanton. Anywhere with school-age density and
strong school districts.

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}}. I'm putting together a shared
> postcard for {{community}} families that goes out via USPS EDDM — about
> {{hh_per_drop}} households around {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I've reserved
> the **pediatric dentistry** slot on the card and {{practice_name}} was
> the first place I thought of."

## Hook

> "Pediatric dentistry on EDDM in {{community}} works because **the postcard
> ends up on the kitchen counter** — exactly where the parent who books the
> appointment sees it. You're not relying on someone seeing your ad in a
> Google search; you're showing up at the moment they're already deciding
> what's for dinner and what's on the calendar."

## The offer

> "Shared postcard — **one pediatric dentist on it, that's you**. Goes to
> about {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} families. Slot is **{{slot_price}}**,
> all-in: design, print, postage, EDDM paperwork, your proof approval.
> 50% holds the slot, 50% before print."

## Pre-objection (the "we get most of our patients from referrals" preempt)

> "I know pediatric dental in {{community}} is mostly referral-driven —
> that's actually why this works. The campaign isn't trying to replace
> referrals. It's trying to give the **new families** moving into
> {{community}} a name and a face the first time they ask 'who do you take
> the kids to?' — so when their neighbor recommends two practices, yours
> is one of them."

## Ask

> "Want me to email the one-pager and the {{community}} delivery footprint,
> or would 10 minutes on a call this week be easier?"

## ROI math

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × 0.4% response = ~24 inquiries
- ~40% schedule = ~10 new pediatric patients
- Average pediatric LTV (8-12 years of cleanings + sealants + early ortho) = $4,500
- New patient revenue per campaign = ~$45,000 over the LTV horizon
- Slot cost {{slot_price}} → 60-80x lifetime ROI per campaign

## Common objections

- **"We don't accept new patients right now"** — great; we'll add a
  waitlist line on the ad and use it to skim future demand.
- **"Schools already know us"** — you'll be reaching the *new* families
  schools haven't introduced yet.
