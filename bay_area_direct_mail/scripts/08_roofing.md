# Pitch script — Roofing

**Best fit communities:** Brentwood, Livermore, Danville (older Blackhawk
stock), Pleasanton (1980s tracts), Fremont 94539 (older-stock pockets).

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}}. I'm putting together a shared
> postcard for {{community}} that goes to about {{hh_per_drop}} households
> around {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I've reserved the **roofing** slot on the
> card and wanted to give {{business_name}} first look."

## Hook

> "Roofing on EDDM in {{community}} works because the housing stock is
> rolling through **the tail end of the 25-year composition cycle right
> now**. We're looking at thousands of {{community}} homes due for re-roof
> in the next 36 months. The postcard catches them at the inspection stage,
> not the emergency stage."

## The offer

> "Shared postcard — **one roofing company on it, that's you**. Going to
> about {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} households. Slot is **{{slot_price}}**,
> all-in: design, print, postage, EDDM paperwork. 50% holds the slot, 50%
> before print. Standard play is a 'free roof inspection + drone report'
> offer — it converts."

## Pre-objection (the "roofing is too expensive of a sale for postcards" preempt)

> "Big-ticket categories like roofing actually do better on a shared
> postcard than small-ticket ones — because **a single closed roof at
> $18-$30K covers the slot 20-40x over**. You don't need a stack of leads,
> you need 1-2 inspections that turn into projects."

## Ask

> "Want me to email the one-pager and the {{community}} delivery footprint,
> or would 10 minutes on a call this week be easier?"

---

## ROI math

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × 0.2% response = ~12 inspection requests
- ~50% qualify for a real estimate = ~6 estimates
- ~25% close = 1-2 roofs
- $22,000 average roof × 1.5 = $33,000 revenue
- Slot cost {{slot_price}} → 35-50x ROI on average campaign

## Common objections

- **"Storm season drives our calls"** — perfect; this fills the
  pre-storm and post-storm shoulder seasons.
- **"We do solar/roof combos"** — even better, lifts AOV substantially.
