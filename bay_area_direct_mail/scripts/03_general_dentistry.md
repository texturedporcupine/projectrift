# Pitch script — General Dentistry

**Best fit communities:** Every Tier 1-2 community. Strongest in family-heavy
zips (San Ramon, Dublin, Pleasanton, south Palo Alto, Fremont 94539).

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}}. I'm pulling together a shared
> postcard for {{community}} families, going to about {{hh_per_drop}} homes
> in and around {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I've reserved the **general
> dentistry** slot on the card and wanted to offer it to {{practice_name}}
> first."

## Hook

> "Dentistry is the highest-LTV vertical on the postcard. A new family
> patient in {{community}} averages **$2,500-$4,000 in the first 18 months**
> — between cleanings, sealants, ortho consults, and the kids. One family
> from the campaign tends to clear the slot cost three to five times over."

## The offer

> "Shared postcard — **one general dentist on it, that's you**. Going to
> about {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} households. Slot is **{{slot_price}}**,
> all-in: design, print, postage, EDDM paperwork, you approve the proof.
> 50% holds the slot, 50% before print."

## Pre-objection (the "we already do Yelp / Google ads" preempt)

> "Most {{community}} dentists I talk to are running Google Ads at $120-$180
> per click. This is closer to **$0.10 per impression to ~6,000 households**
> in the same zip — and exclusivity means {{practice_name}} is the only
> dentist they see when the postcard hits the counter. The two channels
> stack well; this isn't replacing Google."

## Ask

> "Want me to email the one-pager and the {{community}} delivery footprint,
> or would 10 minutes on a call this week be easier?"

---

## ROI math

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × ~0.5% response = ~30 inquiries
- ~30% schedule a new patient visit = ~9 new patients
- 60% of those become families of 3+ over 18 months
- Average family LTV $2,500 → ~$22,500 of new patient revenue per campaign
- Slot cost {{slot_price}} → **20-30x first-year ROI**

## Common objections

- **"We're already busy"** — perfect; growth is about which patients you
  *keep*, and a coordinated piece skews family demographics.
- **"Direct mail doesn't work for dental"** — solo postcards don't; a
  shared piece with category exclusivity does. Different mechanism.
- **"Print is dead"** — at $0.10/HH it's the cheapest channel in your
  marketing mix; happy to walk through the math.
