# Pitch script — Landscape (Maintenance + Design)

**Best fit communities:** Tier 1 — Saratoga, Los Altos Hills, Los Gatos,
Danville (premium design firms). Tier 2 — Pleasanton, San Ramon, Livermore
(maintenance + small redesigns).

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}}. I'm putting together a shared
> postcard for {{community}} that goes to about {{hh_per_drop}} households
> around {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I've reserved the **landscape** slot on
> the card and {{business_name}} was the first call I wanted to make."

## Hook

> "Landscape on EDDM in {{community}} works for two reasons: first, lot
> sizes here are big enough that a single project can be $25K-$80K. Second,
> {{community}} homeowners tend to do a *whole-yard refresh* every 8-12
> years — and the postcard hits a meaningful percentage of yards entering
> that window."

## The offer

> "Shared postcard — **one landscape company on it, that's you**. Going to
> about {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} households. Slot is **{{slot_price}}**,
> all-in: design, print, postage, EDDM paperwork. 50% holds the slot,
> 50% before print."

## Pre-objection (the "we don't need leads, we have a backlog" preempt)

> "If you have a backlog already, this is the moment to **upgrade the
> backlog**. The postcard skews high-end {{community}} addresses, so the
> leads coming in are typically higher-budget than your average referral
> — which means swapping low-margin work for high-margin work without
> growing headcount."

## Ask

> "Want me to email the one-pager and the {{community}} delivery footprint,
> or would 10 minutes on a call this week be easier?"

---

## ROI math (premium tier example)

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × 0.2% response = ~12 high-quality inquiries
- ~20% become consultations = 2-3 consults
- 50% close to project = 1-2 projects
- Average project value $35K → **$35K-$70K revenue per campaign**
- Slot cost {{slot_price}} → 50-100x ROI on a single landed project

## Common objections

- **"Tire-kickers"** — exclusivity + Tier 1 zip skews self-selecting; budget
  minimum on the postcard filters most tire-kickers out.
- **"We're booked through fall"** — perfect, this is for spring/winter projects.
