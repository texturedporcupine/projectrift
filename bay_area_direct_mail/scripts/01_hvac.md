# Pitch script — HVAC

**Best fit communities:** Danville, San Ramon, Pleasanton, Dublin, Fremont,
Livermore. Anywhere with 15-25 year old housing stock + hot inland summers.

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}} — I'm putting together a shared
> postcard for {{community}} families, going to about {{hh_per_drop}}
> homes around {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I'm reaching out to one HVAC
> company in town because I've reserved the HVAC slot on the card and
> wanted to give you first look before I open it up."

## Hook

> "{{community}} has a **lot** of original HVAC equipment from the
> {{community_build_era}} build-out hitting end of life right now — and the
> heat pump rebate environment in California is the strongest it's ever been.
> A piece going to every door at the same time tends to land at exactly the
> moment somebody's system fails."

*Per-community substitution for `{{community_build_era}}`:*

| Community | Era to mention |
|---|---|
| Danville (Blackhawk) | "early-90s and late-80s" |
| San Ramon (Dougherty Valley) | "early 2000s" |
| Pleasanton (Ruby Hill / older Birdland) | "late 80s and 90s" |
| Dublin Ranch | "early 2000s" |
| Livermore | "90s and early 2000s" |

## The offer

> "Here's the deal: it's a single shared postcard, **one HVAC company on it
> — that's you if you want it**. Goes to about {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}}
> households via USPS EDDM. The slot is **{{slot_price}}**, all-in. That
> includes the design, the print, the postage, the EDDM paperwork — you
> approve the proof and I do the rest. **50% deposit holds the slot**, the
> other 50% is due before we print."

## Pre-objection (the "I tried direct mail once" preempt)

> "I know HVAC has been burned by direct mail before — usually because the
> piece was somebody's solo postcard with three competitors' coupons in the
> same week's mail. This is the opposite. **Exclusivity is the whole product**
> — you'll be the only HVAC company on this card. And honestly, in
> {{community}}, **one closed system replacement pays for the slot 8-12x over**.
> You don't need a stack of leads — you need one or two."

## Ask

> "I have the slot held until {{hold_through_date}}. Want me to email you the
> one-pager and the {{community}} delivery footprint, or would a 10-minute
> call this week be easier?"

---

## ROI math to drop in if asked

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × ~0.5% response = ~30 calls / form fills
- ~10% close to a job = 3 jobs
- $9,000 average residential HVAC swap × 3 = $27,000 in revenue
- Slot cost {{slot_price}} → ROI is roughly **30-40x** at average close rate

## Common objections (use objection handler for the full version)

- **"We don't do print"** — fine; this isn't a brochure, it's exclusive
  category share-of-voice in a specific zip for ~$0.10/HH.
- **"How do I track it?"** — dedicated phone number on the card; we hand it
  to a tracking provider so every call rings through to you with attribution.
- **"I need to think about it"** — totally fair; the only thing I'd ask is
  whether to hold the slot or release it. I won't sell you twice.
