# Pitch script — Pool Service

**Best fit communities:** Danville (Blackhawk has heavy pool density), Pleasanton
(Ruby Hill), Los Gatos, Saratoga, Brentwood, Livermore. Anywhere with big lots
+ inland summers.

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}}. I'm putting together a shared
> postcard going to about {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} households around
> {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I've reserved the **pool service** slot on the
> card and wanted to give {{business_name}} first look."

## Hook

> "Pool service on EDDM in {{community}} works because the
> {{anchor_neighborhood}} pool density is high and **most homeowners are not
> happy with whoever they currently use** — chemistry's off, the tech is
> always late, the bill keeps creeping. The postcard hits at the moment
> they're considering switching."

## The offer

> "Shared postcard — **one pool service on it, that's you**. Goes to about
> {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} households. Slot is **{{slot_price}}**,
> all-in: design, print, postage, EDDM paperwork. 50% holds the slot,
> 50% before print. Standard pitch is a 'first month free with quarterly
> service' offer — converts well in this category."

## Pre-objection (the "we're already at capacity" preempt)

> "If you're at capacity, this is *more* useful, not less — every new
> {{community}} customer goes to **monthly recurring**, which means every
> swap of a low-tip / high-effort customer for a {{community}} customer
> upgrades your route. Saying yes today doesn't mean adding stress; it
> means upgrading the book."

## Ask

> "Want me to email the one-pager and the {{community}} delivery footprint,
> or would 10 minutes on a call this week be easier?"

---

## ROI math

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × ~0.3% response (high homeowner-with-pool conversion) = ~18 inquiries
- ~25% close to monthly service = 4-5 new monthly accounts
- Average $200/mo × 12 mo × 4 accounts = $9,600 first-year recurring
- Slot cost {{slot_price}} → **12-16x first-year ROI**

## Common objections

- **"It's seasonal here"** — even in the Bay Area pool customers are
  year-round in {{community}}; we'll target spring drop.
- **"We tried postcards"** — solo postcards don't work; shared postcards
  with category exclusivity have a different conversion mechanism.
