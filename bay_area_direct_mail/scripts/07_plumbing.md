# Pitch script — Plumbing

**Best fit communities:** All Tier 1-2. Strongest in older-stock zips
(Pleasanton 94566, Danville 94526, parts of Los Gatos / Saratoga, Palo Alto).
Older copper + galvanized lines = repipe + water heater swap demand.

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}}. I'm putting together a shared
> postcard for {{community}} households — about {{hh_per_drop}} homes around
> {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I've reserved the **plumbing** slot on the card
> and wanted to give {{business_name}} first look before I open it up."

## Hook

> "Plumbing in {{community}} is one of the most reliable EDDM categories
> because **water heaters fail on their own schedule**. The postcard ends
> up on the fridge or in the kitchen drawer — and four to six months later
> when the water heater dies, **your name is the first one they see**.
> That's a 'boring' but very high ROI dynamic."

## The offer

> "Shared postcard — **one plumbing company on it, that's you**. Going to
> about {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} households. Slot is **{{slot_price}}**,
> all-in: design, print, postage, EDDM paperwork. 50% holds the slot,
> 50% before print. Most plumbing slots use a 'water heater inspection +
> emergency response' offer because that converts on the actual moment of need."

## Pre-objection (the "we get most calls from Google" preempt)

> "Google-driven plumbing leads in {{community}} are running $80-$150 per
> click in the local pack. The postcard is closer to **$0.10 per impression**
> over 6,000 households — and on a tankless install or a repipe, **one
> closed job pays for the slot 8-15x over**. The two channels stack;
> Google catches the 'right now' calls, the postcard catches the 'when it
> happens' calls."

## Ask

> "Want me to email the one-pager and the {{community}} delivery footprint,
> or would 10 minutes on a call this week be easier?"

---

## ROI math

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × 0.4% response = ~24 inquiries (over the 90-day life of the postcard)
- ~30% close to a service call = ~7 jobs
- $750 average ticket × 7 = $5,250 revenue
- Plus 1-2 high-ticket water heater / repipe jobs at $4-8K = additional $4-16K
- Slot cost {{slot_price}} → 10-25x ROI

## Common objections

- **"We don't need more emergency calls"** — switch the offer to a
  plumbing inspection / scheduled work program; same slot, different angle.
- **"Mail is dead"** — at $0.10/HH it's the cheapest channel in your mix.
