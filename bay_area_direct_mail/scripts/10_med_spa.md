# Pitch script — Med Spa / Aesthetics

**Best fit communities:** Tier 1 — Saratoga, Los Gatos, Los Altos Hills,
Danville, Palo Alto, Piedmont. The single highest-ROI vertical on the postcard
in the right zip.

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}}. I'm putting together a shared
> postcard for {{community}} families and homeowners — about {{hh_per_drop}}
> households around {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I've reserved the **med spa**
> slot on the card and wanted to offer it to {{business_name}} first."

## Hook

> "Med spa is the **single highest-ROI vertical** on a {{tier}} postcard.
> {{community}} households over-index on disposable income and on
> aesthetic-services demand — and exclusivity means **{{business_name}}
> is the only med spa they see when the card hits the counter**. There is
> no other med spa on the piece. That's the whole product."

## The offer

> "Shared postcard — **one med spa on it, that's you**. Goes to about
> {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} households. Standard slot is
> **{{slot_price}}**, but med spa is the most common **premium-placement**
> upsell — for an extra ~50% you get the back panel anchor, which is the
> first thing the recipient sees when the postcard hits the counter."

## Pre-objection (the "we already do Instagram" preempt)

> "Instagram targets a *bigger* funnel — broader reach, lower intent. The
> postcard targets a *smaller, denser* funnel — every home in the zip,
> with the highest-disposable-income households over-represented. The
> two stack well; this isn't a replacement, it's a precision channel."

## Ask

> "Want me to email the one-pager and the {{community}} delivery footprint,
> or would 10 minutes on a call this week be easier? Either way, I'd
> recommend grabbing **premium placement** for this category specifically."

---

## ROI math (premium placement on a Tier 1 mailer)

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × 0.6% response (med spa over-indexes) = ~36 inquiries
- ~30% book a consult = ~11 consults
- ~60% convert to a first treatment = ~7 first treatments
- Average first-visit ticket $400 × 7 = $2,800
- LTV (botox + filler + laser cycles) = $4,500/customer × 7 = **$31,500**
- Slot cost (premium) {{premium_slot_price}} → 25-40x lifetime ROI

## Common objections

- **"We're more luxury than the postcard format suggests"** — the design
  is fully under your control; we'll bias the layout to a premium photo
  and tasteful type. You approve every proof.
- **"Most of our growth is referrals"** — same answer as pediatric
  dentistry: the postcard catches new {{community}} arrivals before
  referrals reach them.
- **"We have a competitor on the corner"** — perfect, because they
  *won't* be on the postcard. That's the whole product.
