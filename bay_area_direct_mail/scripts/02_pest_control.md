# Pitch script — Pest Control

**Best fit communities:** Danville, San Ramon, Pleasanton, Dublin, Livermore,
Brentwood, Fremont. Anywhere with rodent/spider/ant pressure (i.e. all of them).

---

## Open (15 sec)

> "Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{your_name}}. I'm putting a shared postcard
> together for {{community}} — one going to about {{hh_per_drop}} homes
> across {{anchor_neighborhood}}. I've got the **pest control slot** open
> and wanted to give you first look before I open it to anyone else."

## Hook

> "Pest control on EDDM in {{community}} is one of the strongest direct mail
> verticals out there because the **lifetime value is recurring**. You don't
> need to close 30 jobs — you need to convert ~5 quarterly contracts and
> the slot pays for itself for the next two years."

## The offer

> "It's a shared postcard — **one pest control company, that's you**.
> Goes to about {{hh_per_drop}} {{community}} households. Slot is
> **{{slot_price}}**, all-in: design, print, postage, EDDM paperwork.
> 50% deposit holds the slot, 50% before we print."

## Pre-objection (the "I'm already booked out"  preempt)

> "If you're already booked out — even better. Quarterly contract programs
> tend to lock people in for years. So we'd lead with the recurring program
> on the postcard, not a one-shot service call. The campaign builds your
> contract base, not your one-and-dones."

## Ask

> "Should I email you the one-pager and the {{community}} delivery
> footprint, or would 10 minutes on the phone this week be easier?"

---

## ROI math

- {{hh_per_drop}} HHs × ~0.5% response = ~30 inquiries
- ~15% close to a quarterly contract = 4-5 contracts
- $400/yr quarterly contract × 5 = $2,000 first-year revenue
- ...and ~70% of those renew = $1,400/yr in years 2+
- Slot cost {{slot_price}} → **3-year ROI on quarterly contracts alone: 8-12x**

## Common objections

- **"We're not adding new clients"** — even better, this fills your
  recurring book; if it overflows, you can refer out.
- **"Pest is too commodity"** — exclusivity makes you the only quote in
  someone's mailbox; that's the opposite of commodity.
- **"Print is dead"** — the math at $0.10/HH says otherwise; we can
  walk through it together.
