## 1. The audience

- **Households reached this drop:** ~{{hh_per_drop}}
- **Median HHI:** {{med_hhi}}
- **Homeownership:** {{homeownership}}
- **Anchor neighborhoods:** {{anchor_neighborhoods}}
- **What they care about:** quality > price, referral-driven decision making,
  long-time-horizon services (kids' dentist, recurring HVAC, family pool),
  short tolerance for tackiness.

## 2. The voice

- **Tone:** confident, neighborly, premium. Not corporate.
- **Avoid:** stock-photo couples laughing at salads, exclamation point
  inflation, "act now!", giant red sale starbursts.
- **Visual reference:** clean magazine ads in Sunset, Diablo Magazine,
  or local HOA newsletters. Generous white space. One hero photo per ad
  block, not three.

## 3. Per-ad block content (collect from each advertiser)

Each advertiser provides:

- High-resolution **logo** (vector preferred, PNG transparent acceptable)
- 1-2 line **headline** (e.g., "Your {{community}} family dentist since 2009")
- 1-2 sentence **offer** (e.g., "New patient cleaning + exam: $99")
- **Phone**, **website URL**, **address**, optional **QR code**
- 1 **hero photo** (real, not stock — owner, team, or work product)
- Required **disclaimers** if applicable (NMLS, dental board, etc.)

## 4. Layout principles

- **Card front:** 1 hero advertiser (anchor, premium placement) + community
  / route headline + 2-3 secondary ad blocks + EDDM indicia.
- **Card back:** address area (left), 4-5 standard ad blocks (right), small
  back-page anchor block.
- **Grid:** 4-column on the back; vary blocks 1-3 columns wide for visual
  rhythm.
- **Typography:** one display family (sans), one body (humanist sans or
  text serif). No more than 2 type families across the entire piece.

## 5. EDDM compliance checklist

- [ ] Postcard size meets EDDM Retail flat dimensions (>6.125" tall OR >10.5"
      long; max 12" x 15")
- [ ] EDDM indicia placed in the upper-right of the address-side panel
- [ ] White address-area free of art / text (USPS auto-detection requirement)
- [ ] Return address printed, optional but recommended
- [ ] Cardstock 14pt or thicker, 0.007" minimum
- [ ] Printer is EDDM-aware (PrintingForLess, GotPrint, 48HourPrint, PrintRunner)

## 6. Approval workflow

1. Provider produces **proof v1** within 5 business days of receiving all
   creative inputs.
2. Each advertiser reviews **only their ad block** and approves or
   requests changes (≤2 rounds included in slot price).
3. Provider produces **proof v2** within 3 business days of revision requests.
4. **Final sign-off** required from every advertiser in writing before
   release to print.
5. Printer turnaround: 5-7 business days.
6. Drop at DDU on Drop Date.

## 7. Tracking recommendations (per advertiser)

- **Phone:** dedicated tracking number (CallRail or similar) — strongly
  recommended.
- **URL:** unique landing-page URL or UTM-tagged URL — recommended.
- **QR code:** linking to the same UTM URL — optional but raises
  attribution by ~30%.
