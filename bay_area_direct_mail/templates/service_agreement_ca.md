## 1. Services

Provider will design, produce, and deliver one (1) advertising placement on
a shared direct-mail postcard ("**Postcard**") delivered via USPS Every
Door Direct Mail (EDDM) to households in **{{community}}, CA (zip code(s)
{{zip_codes}})** ("**Campaign**") on or about **{{drop_date}}**
("**Drop Date**").

The Services include: creative design and layout of Advertiser's ad block;
two (2) rounds of revisions; coordination of print production with a
qualified commercial printer; preparation of EDDM facing slips and USPS
Form 3587; delivery to the Destination Delivery Unit (DDU) serving the
Campaign zip code(s); and confirmation of delivery to Advertiser.

## 2. Category Exclusivity

Provider shall not include any other business in Advertiser's category
on the Postcard for the Campaign. Advertiser's category for purposes of
this Agreement is: **{{advertiser_category}}**.

The exclusivity granted in this Section 2 applies only to the specific
Campaign defined in Section 1 and does not extend to any other Campaign
unless renewed in writing.

## 3. Fees and Payment

**Total Fee:** **${{total_fee}}** (USD), all-inclusive of design, two
revision rounds, print, EDDM postage, and DDU delivery.

**Payment Schedule:**

- **50% deposit** of **${{deposit_amount}}** due upon execution of this
  Agreement. This payment locks Advertiser's category exclusivity for the
  Campaign.
- **50% balance** of **${{balance_amount}}** due no later than **seven (7)
  calendar days** prior to the Drop Date, and in any event prior to print
  release.

Late payments accrue interest at 1.0% per month from the due date.

## 4. Proof Approval

Provider will deliver an initial proof of the Postcard to Advertiser
within **five (5) business days** after Advertiser delivers all required
creative inputs (logo, offer, phone, URL, address, photos, and any
required disclaimers).

Advertiser shall review and either approve or request revisions in writing
within **three (3) business days**. Failure to respond within ten (10)
business days will be deemed approval.

**Once Advertiser approves a proof in writing, no further changes may be
made**, and Provider may release the Postcard to print.

## 5. Cancellation and Refunds

- **Pre-proof cancellation:** Advertiser may cancel prior to first proof
  delivery and receive a refund of **75% of the deposit**. The remaining
  25% covers Provider's category-lock and design preparation costs.
- **Post-proof cancellation:** No refund. Print and postage costs are
  committed by Provider once a proof is approved.
- **Provider termination:** Provider may terminate this Agreement and
  refund the full deposit if (i) fewer than four (4) total advertisers
  sign for the Campaign, or (ii) the EDDM routes for {{community}} become
  unavailable for the Drop Date.

## 6. Advertiser Content & Indemnification

Advertiser represents and warrants that all content provided by
Advertiser (including text, logos, images, offers, and claims) is
accurate, non-infringing, and compliant with applicable law (including
but not limited to FTC, USPS, and California Business and Professions
Code requirements).

Advertiser will indemnify and hold Provider harmless from any claim,
damage, or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from
Advertiser's content or from any product or service offered or provided
by Advertiser to a Campaign recipient.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

Provider's aggregate liability for any claim arising under this
Agreement is limited to the **Total Fee** actually paid by Advertiser
under this Agreement. Provider is not liable for indirect, incidental,
consequential, or punitive damages, including but not limited to lost
profits or lost business opportunity. Some jurisdictions do not allow
limitations of liability for personal injury or other categories — to
the extent applicable law prohibits a particular limitation, that
limitation does not apply.

Provider does not guarantee any specific response rate, lead volume,
revenue, or return on investment from the Campaign.

## 8. Independent Contractor

Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates
an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship between
the Parties.

## 9. Intellectual Property

Final design files of the Postcard are the property of Provider.
Advertiser's logos and brand assets remain the property of Advertiser
and are licensed to Provider solely for use in the Campaign.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep confidential any non-public information of the other
Party disclosed during the Campaign, including pricing, customer lists,
and the identity of other advertisers on the Postcard.

without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. The exclusive venue for
any dispute is the state or federal courts located in
**{{provider_county}} County, California**, and each Party consents to
personal jurisdiction in those courts.

matter and supersedes any prior or contemporaneous agreements,
