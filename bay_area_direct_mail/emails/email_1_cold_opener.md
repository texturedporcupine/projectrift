# Email 1 — Cold opener

**Audience:** A specific local business in your target community.
**Goal:** Get a 10-minute reply or a phone callback. Not a sale. Not a meeting yet.
**Best time to send:** Tue-Thu, 7:30-9:00 AM local.

---

**Subject lines (A/B):**

- A: `Quick idea for {{business_name}} — {{community}} families`
- B: `One-of-a-kind {{community}} mailer — saved {{category}} slot for you`

---

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

I'm putting together a shared neighborhood postcard going to about
{{hh_per_drop}} households in **{{community}} ({{zip}})** in the next
{{weeks_until_drop}} weeks. It's an EDDM piece — every door on the routes
serving the **{{anchor_neighborhood}}** area gets one.

The idea is simple: **one business per category** on the card. One HVAC, one
dentist, one pool service, one landscaper — no competing businesses on the same
piece. That's the whole pitch.

I noticed {{business_name}} on {{corridor}} and you'd be the {{category}} spot
on the card if you want it. Before I open the slot to anyone else, I wanted
to give you first look.

A few quick reasons it tends to work in {{community}}:

- High homeownership and active HOAs — people actually read the mail.
- {{schools_or_anchor_hook}} drives long-term, repeat-customer households.
- Postage is $0.247/piece via USPS EDDM — extremely efficient on a per-impression basis.
- Exclusivity means your ad isn't competing with the other 3 {{category}} businesses on the same card.

Happy to send over a one-page summary with pricing and the {{community}}
delivery footprint. Want me to?

Thanks,
{{your_name}}
{{your_phone}}
{{your_email}}
{{your_url}}

## Send rules

- **Always personalize the corridor + anchor.** Generic cold emails die.
- **Never send to more than 25 prospects in a single morning.** Looks spammy
  to inbox providers and to the local market.
- **Reply within 1 business day** to any response — interest decays fast.
