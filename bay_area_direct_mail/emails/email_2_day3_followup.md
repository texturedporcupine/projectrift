# Email 2 — Day 3 follow-up

**Audience:** Anyone who didn't reply to Email 1.
**Goal:** Lower the friction. Add a concrete number. Offer a 10-minute call.
**Send window:** 72 hours after Email 1 (skip weekends).

---

**Subject lines (A/B):**

- A: `Re: Quick idea for {{business_name}} — {{community}} families`
  *(reply in same thread, do NOT change the subject)*
- B: `{{community}} {{category}} slot — 2 quick numbers`

---

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

Quick bump on the {{community}} postcard. I'll keep it short.

Two numbers most {{category}} owners care about:

1. **Cost per impression:** about **$0.{{cpm_cents}}** per household reached
   ({{hh_per_drop}} HHs ÷ ${{slot_price}}). Cheaper than basically every

   Everything after that is a year of margin.

"hold" and I'll keep it open through {{hold_through_date}}.

If now isn't the right time, I'll close it out and try again next campaign —
no pressure either way.

10-minute call this week works too. Easiest slot for you?

Thanks,
{{your_name}}
{{your_phone}}

## Send rules

- **Reply in the same thread** as Email 1 — do not start a new thread.
- **Never push a meeting harder than once per email.** Two soft asks across
  two emails > one hard ask.
- **If they reply "not now,"** that's a soft yes for the next campaign.
  Move them to **MAYBE** in the tracker and re-add to the next outreach wave.
