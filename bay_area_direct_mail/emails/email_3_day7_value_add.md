# Email 3 — Day 7 value-add (close-or-bow-out)

**Audience:** Anyone who didn't reply to Emails 1 or 2.
**Goal:** Provide a piece of standalone value (so even if they say no this
campaign, the relationship is positive). Then quietly close the loop.
**Send window:** 4 days after Email 2 (so day 7 from first contact).

---

**Subject lines (A/B):**

- A: `Re: Quick idea for {{business_name}} — {{community}} families`
- B: `Quick {{community}} demographics for your records`

---

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

Last note from me on the {{community}} postcard — I won't pester you again on it.

Whether or not the timing is right for you, here are the {{community}}
demographics I pulled while building this campaign. They're useful for any
direct response work you do here:

- **{{community}} ({{zip}}):** ~{{homes}} households, median HHI {{med_hhi}},
  homeownership ~{{homeownership}}.
- **Tier:** {{tier_name}}. Strongest {{category}} demand drivers:
  {{demand_drivers}}.
- **Anchor neighborhoods:** {{anchor_neighborhood}}.
- **EDDM cost benchmark:** $0.247/piece postage + ~$0.06/piece print at
  6,000 pieces. Total in-the-mail cost is roughly **$0.31/HH** — useful as
  a benchmark whether you ever do a solo mailer or a shared one.

I'll close the {{category}} slot on this campaign by **{{close_date}}** and open
it to one other business in your category. If you want to grab it before then,
just reply "in" and I'll send the agreement.

If now isn't the time, no worries — I'll circle back when I run the next
{{community}} campaign in roughly {{next_campaign_window}}.

Thanks for your time, {{first_name}} — appreciated.

{{your_name}}
{{your_phone}}
{{your_email}}

---

## Why this email works

- It **gives standalone value** even if they say no — leaves a positive
  impression for the next outreach.
- It **establishes a real deadline** without manufacturing fake scarcity.
- It **makes "no for now" easy** — which counterintuitively unlocks more
  yeses next campaign.

---

## After Email 3

| Outcome | What to do |
|---|---|
| No reply at all | Mark **MAYBE** in tracker. Add to next campaign's outreach list (8-10 weeks out). |
| "Not this campaign, ping me next time" | Mark **STRONG** in tracker, set Last Touch + Next Action = "ping for next campaign". |
| "Send me the agreement" | Move stage -> **Verbal yes**. Send agreement within 4 hours. |
| "Too expensive" | Use the universal objection handler in `sales/objection_handler.md`. |
