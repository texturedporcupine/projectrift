# SF Bay Area Communities Guide

> Companion to `bay_area_business_plan.md`. Use this to pick the right community
> for each campaign and to brief design/copy decisions.

The Bay Area has an abundance of high-income master-planned and established
suburban communities that fit the model perfectly. Prioritize communities with:

- Strong, active HOAs (residents read community mail)
- Clear geographic boundaries (zip-aligned EDDM routes)
- High homeownership rate (>70%)
- Dense local-business ecosystem in adjacent commercial corridors

## Tiering Cheat Sheet

| Tier | Median HHI | Standard ad space | Premium placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Premium** | $200K+ | $700-900 | $1,100-1,400 |
| **Tier 2 — Strong** | $140K-200K | $550-750 | $850-1,100 |
| **Tier 3 — Growth** | $100K-140K | $400-600 | $650-850 |

> First-campaign targets: **Danville (94526)** or **San Ramon (94582/94583)** —
> both have the strongest community-mail-reading culture in the East Bay,
> analogous to Circle C Ranch in the Austin playbook.

## Community Profiles (deep-dive)

### 1. Danville (94526) — TIER 1, FIRST-CAMPAIGN PICK

- **Median HHI:** $185K+ | **Homeownership:** ~80% | **Homes:** 18,000+
- **Character:** Affluent, established, golf-and-equestrian East Bay community.
  Blackhawk, Diablo Country Club, downtown Hartz Ave. corridor. Strong "shop
  local" identity. Active HOAs in Blackhawk, Crow Canyon Country Club, and
  several master-planned subdivisions.
- **Mail-reading culture:** **High.** Multiple community newsletters
  (Blackhawk Living, Alamo Today, Diablo Magazine), low transient population,
  high homeownership.
- **EDDM viability:** 3-5 high-quality routes around 94526. Filter eddm.usps.com
  for HHI $150K+, HH size 2.5+. Expect 1,500-2,200 deliverable HHs per route.
- **Recommended categories:** HVAC, landscaping (premium), pool service, general
  dentistry, med spa/aesthetics, luxury home renovation, pediatric dentistry.
- **Pricing:** Tier 1. Standard $700-900, premium back-cover $1,100-1,400.
- **Local hooks for pitch scripts:**
  - "Serving Blackhawk and Diablo Country Club families since [year]"
  - "From Sycamore Valley to downtown Hartz Ave."
  - "Trusted across the Tri-Valley for over [N] years"
- **Top commercial corridors to prospect:** Hartz Ave (downtown), Crow Canyon
  Rd, Camino Tassajara, San Ramon Valley Blvd, Diablo Rd.

---

### 2. San Ramon (94582 / 94583) — TIER 1-2, FIRST-CAMPAIGN ALT

- **Median HHI:** $160K+ | **Homeownership:** ~73% | **Homes:** 30,000+
- **Character:** Master-planned, tech-employed family demographic. Bishop Ranch
  business park anchors the south end; Dougherty Valley and Windemere are the
  newer master-planned tracts in 94582. Excellent schools (SRVUSD).
- **Mail-reading culture:** **Medium-high.** Master-planned tracts with active
  HOAs receive HOA mailings, so a coordinated EDDM piece reads as
  community-curated rather than junk.
- **EDDM viability:** 4-7 routes across 94582/94583. Dougherty Valley alone is
  ~12,000 households.
- **Recommended categories:** HVAC, K-12 tutoring/test prep, family dentist,
  pest control, pediatric dentistry, house cleaning, pool service, orthodontics.
- **Pricing:** Tier 2 (some Tier 1 in 94582 Dougherty Valley). Standard $550-800.
- **Local hooks:**
  - "Trusted by Dougherty Valley and Windemere families"
  - "Right off Crow Canyon — minutes from your door"
  - "Serving the SRVUSD community"
- **Commercial corridors:** Crow Canyon Rd, Bollinger Canyon Rd, Bishop Ranch,
  San Ramon Valley Blvd, Alcosta Blvd.

---

### 3. Pleasanton (94566 / 94588) — TIER 2

- **Median HHI:** $155K+ | **Homeownership:** ~70% | **Homes:** 28,000+
- **Character:** Established Tri-Valley suburbs with strong downtown Main St.
  identity. Ruby Hill (gated, $1.5M+ homes) anchors the upper end of 94566;
  Birdland and the wineries-area neighborhoods are mid-tier.
- **Mail-reading culture:** **Medium-high** in Ruby Hill and Castlewood;
  medium in newer tract communities.
- **EDDM viability:** 5-8 routes total across both zips. Best to pick by
  neighborhood (e.g., Ruby Hill alone) rather than blanket the whole city.
- **Recommended categories:** Pool service, landscape design, orthodontics,
  general dentistry, house cleaning, HVAC, med spa.
- **Pricing:** Tier 2. Standard $550-750. Ruby Hill specifically supports Tier 1.
- **Commercial corridors:** Main Street downtown, Stoneridge Mall area,
  Bernal Ave, Hopyard Rd, Santa Rita Rd.

---

### 4. Los Gatos (95030 / 95032) — TIER 1

- **Median HHI:** $200K+ | **Homeownership:** ~70% | **Homes:** 12,000+
- **Character:** Affluent South Bay with a charming downtown (Santa Cruz Ave),
  hillside estates, and tech-executive families. High disposable income,
  high preference for premium services.
- **EDDM viability:** 4-5 routes. Filter for HHI $200K+ if possible.
- **Recommended categories:** Med spa, luxury home renovation, pool service,
  general/cosmetic dentistry, premium landscaping, concierge home services.
- **Pricing:** Tier 1. Standard $700-900, premium $1,100-1,400.
- **Commercial corridors:** Santa Cruz Ave (downtown), Los Gatos Blvd,
  University Ave, Blossom Hill Rd.

---

### 5. Saratoga (95070) — TIER 1 PREMIUM

- **Median HHI:** $250K+ | **Homeownership:** ~83% | **Homes:** 10,000+
- **Character:** One of the wealthiest cities in CA. Old-money + tech wealth.
  Quiet, residential, very high homeownership. Smaller market — fewer
  households means each ad reaches a denser high-net-worth audience.
- **EDDM viability:** 3-4 routes. Lower HH counts, higher conversion economics.
- **Recommended categories:** Luxury home renovation, med spa, estate planning,
  premium landscape architects, fine jewelry, private wine retailers.
- **Pricing:** **Tier 1 Premium.** Standard $850-1,100, premium $1,400-1,800.
  Many advertisers will pay a premium for *exclusivity* in this demographic.
- **Commercial corridors:** Big Basin Way, Saratoga-Sunnyvale Rd, Saratoga Ave.

### 6. Los Altos / Los Altos Hills (94022 / 94024) — TIER 1 PREMIUM

- **Median HHI:** $300K+ | **Homeownership:** ~85%+ | **Homes:** ~8,000
- **Character:** Among the highest median incomes in the country. Tech
  C-suite, founders, partners, retired executives. Smaller deliverable
  universe — premium per-piece economics.
- **EDDM viability:** 2-3 routes. Treat each route as a curated mailer.
- **Recommended categories:** Concierge medicine, premium landscape architects,
  custom home builders, estate attorneys, art advisors, luxury auto detailing.
- **Pricing:** Tier 1 Premium. Standard $900-1,200, premium $1,500-2,000.

## Community Research Process (run before each new campaign)

1. **eddm.usps.com** — enter target zip, filter HHI $100K+ and HH size 2.5+.
   Screenshot 2-3 viable routes; record household counts and route IDs.
2. **HOA check** — Google `[community name] HOA`. Active HOA = mail-reading
   culture. Note newsletter publisher names.
3. **Local news / newsletter** — Check Nextdoor presence, Patch articles, the
   community paper. If a neighborhood newsletter exists, that is gold.
4. **Commercial corridors** — Use Google Maps to scout the 2-3 commercial
   corridors that serve the neighborhood. Those are the streets your
   advertisers' storefronts are on.
5. **Build the prospect list** — 30-40 businesses across 8-10 non-competing
   categories *before* any outreach. Use `prospects/prospect-tracker_TEMPLATE.xlsx`.
