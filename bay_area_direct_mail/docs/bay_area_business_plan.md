# SF Bay Area Shared Postcard Direct Mail — Business Plan (CA)

> Adapted from the Austin TX playbook. Sections 1 (Legal/Tax) and 2
> (Communities) are rewritten for California; the remainder is largely
> market-neutral with Bay Area pricing applied.

## 0. Executive Summary

A direct-mail advertising business that sells **exclusive ad space to local
businesses on a shared, professionally designed postcard**, then uses **USPS
Every Door Direct Mail (EDDM)** to blanket a target Bay Area neighborhood
(5,000-8,000 households per drop). One advertiser per category per postcard —
no competing dentists, no competing HVAC companies on the same card.

- **Average campaign:** 6-8 advertisers @ $500-900 each = $3,000-7,200 revenue
- **Costs:** ~$350 printing + ~$1,400 EDDM postage + $100 misc = **~$1,850**
- **Target net per campaign:** $1,500-4,000 (depending on fill rate and tier)
- **Cadence at maturity:** 1 campaign / 2 weeks across Tri-Valley + South Bay

## 1. Legal & Tax (California)

> **Important:** California rules differ meaningfully from Texas. Do not reuse
> the TX legal section. The items below are the minimum to launch as a sole
> proprietor in any Bay Area county (Alameda, Contra Costa, Santa Clara,
> San Mateo, Marin). Always confirm with your county recorder, your city
> business-license office, and the CA CDTFA / FTB before relying on them.

### 1.1 Business structure

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Sole proprietorship** (recommended to start) | Simplest, no entity tax. Just a DBA. | No liability shield. |
| Single-member LLC | Liability shield, professional perception. | **CA $800/year minimum franchise tax** regardless of revenue. |
| S-Corp | Tax efficiency at higher revenue. | Adds payroll, accounting overhead. |

> Start as a sole prop with a DBA. Convert to an LLC once you are running
> 3-4 campaigns/month and want the liability separation.

### 1.2 DBA / Fictitious Business Name (FBN)

In California a DBA is called a **Fictitious Business Name (FBN)** statement.

- File with your **county recorder** (not the county clerk — common confusion
  point migrating from Texas).
- **Cost:** $26-$100 depending on county.
- **Publish** the FBN in an *adjudicated* local newspaper for **4 consecutive
  weeks**, **within 30 days** of filing. Budget $50-150 for publication.
- Keep the **affidavit of publication** on file.
- **Renew every 5 years.**

| County | Recorder cost (approx.) | Adjudicated paper examples |
|---|---|---|
| Alameda | $40 + $7/extra name | Inter-City Express, East Bay Times |
| Contra Costa | $55 first business name | Brentwood Press, Rossmoor News, Lamorinda Weekly |
| Santa Clara | $46 first business name | San Jose Post-Record, Saratoga News, Los Gatos Weekly-Times |
| San Mateo | $47 first business name | The Daily Journal, San Mateo Daily News |
| Marin | $58 first business name | Marin Independent Journal, Pacific Sun |

### 1.3 Business license

- California has **no statewide** business license.
- Most CA cities/counties require a **local business license** ($50-$200/year).
  Check the city you operate in (where you receive mail/work — not necessarily
  the city you market into).
- For a home-based sole prop, this is generally a quick online application.

### 1.4 Sales & use tax

California treats *advertising services* differently from *printed materials*:

- **Advertising services themselves are generally not taxable** (CDTFA Pub 17).
- **Printed materials sold to clients** can be taxable depending on the
  **transfer of title** model:
  - If you contract with the printer and pay print costs as *your* expense,
    and you sell the *advertising service* to the customer, the print is a
    cost-of-goods, not a taxable item to the customer.
  - If you separately bill the printed cards as a deliverable to the customer,
    that may be a taxable retail sale of tangible personal property.
- **Action:** Keep your service agreement framed as "advertising space and
  campaign management services" with print as a cost component, not a
  separately invoiced product. Confirm with the **CA CDTFA** (cdtfa.ca.gov).
- If you cross the **economic nexus thresholds**, register for a CDTFA seller's
  permit. As a service business under $500K/yr in retail sales, this is rarely
  triggered, but verify.

### 1.5 Income & franchise tax

- **Sole prop**: report on Schedule C of your federal 1040 + CA Form 540.
- **LLC**: $800/yr minimum franchise tax to CA FTB regardless of revenue.
  First year may be waived if formed in Q4. Plus a per-revenue-tier LLC fee
  above $250K gross receipts.
- **No CA estimated tax exemption** — quarterly estimates are required once
  expected tax exceeds ~$500.

### 1.6 Insurance

- **General liability**: $300-$600/yr through Hiscox, Next, or Thimble.
  Required by some clients and recommended for any in-person sales activity.
- **Professional liability (E&O)**: optional; $400-$800/yr. Useful once you
  manage >$10K of campaign budget on behalf of customers.

### 1.7 Banking & payments

- Open a **dedicated business checking** (no need for a registered LLC —
  most banks accept a sole-prop with FBN + EIN).
- Get an **EIN** from irs.gov (free, ~5 minutes online).
- Accept payment via **ACH (Stripe ACH or direct), credit card (Stripe), or
  check**. Avoid cash for >$1K transactions.

### 1.8 Compliance checklist (pre-launch)

- [ ] FBN filed with county recorder
- [ ] FBN published 4 weeks; affidavit on file
- [ ] City business license issued
- [ ] EIN issued
- [ ] Business checking account opened
- [ ] Stripe account live (or chosen processor)
- [ ] General liability policy bound
- [ ] Service agreement template reviewed by a CA attorney (~$300-$500 flat fee)

## 3. Pricing Model

| Tier | Standard ad space | Premium placement |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Premium** (Danville, Los Gatos, Saratoga, Los Altos, Palo Alto, Piedmont) | $700-$900 | $1,100-$1,400 |
| **Tier 2 — Strong** (San Ramon, Pleasanton, Dublin) | $550-$750 | $850-$1,100 |
| **Tier 3 — Growth** (Fremont, Livermore, Brentwood, Rocklin/Roseville) | $400-$600 | $650-$850 |

Bay Area pricing is set **20-40% above Austin** to reflect higher local
business revenue, higher cost-of-living expectations, and higher CAC values
in Tri-Valley / South Bay markets.

### Discounts & packaging

- **Multi-campaign discount:** 15-20% off slot 2 if booked at signing for
  campaign 1.
- **Annual partner:** 10% off all slots, locked rate, right of first refusal
  on category exclusivity.
- **Premium placement:** back panel + larger ad block + first-page mention =
  Tier 1 multiplier on the standard rate.
- **No agency / referral discounts** in year 1 — protect margin.

### Payment terms

- **50% deposit** at signing (locks the category exclusivity)
- **50% due before printing** (typically 7-10 days before drop date)
- **No refunds** after design proof approval; pre-proof refunds are 75% of deposit

## 4. Campaign Workflow

1. **Pick a community** (use the communities guide). Lock 2-3 EDDM carrier
   routes totaling 5,000-7,000 households.
2. **Build prospect list** of 30-40 businesses across 8-10 non-competing
   categories. Use `prospects/prospect-tracker_<community>-<zip>.xlsx`.
3. **Outreach**: Email sequence (cold / day-3 / day-7) -> phone follow-up ->
   in-person for high-value targets.
4. **Sell** category-exclusive slots. Collect 50% deposit, signed agreement.
5. **Design** the postcard. Send proofs to all advertisers; require sign-off
   before final print.
6. **Print** with a flat-mail-spec-compliant printer (PrintingForLess, GotPrint,
   48HourPrint, or local PrintRunner).
7. **EDDM paperwork**: complete USPS Form 3587, prep facing slips, bundle.
8. **Drop at DDU** serving the target zip(s). Pay $0.247/piece.
9. **Confirm delivery** with USPS within 1-3 days of drop.
10. **Follow up** with advertisers at 2 weeks. Collect testimonials, leads
    counts, ROI data. Pitch renewal at multi-campaign discount.

## 5. Financial Projections

### First-campaign worst/expected/best

| Scenario | Filled slots | Avg price | Revenue | Costs | Net |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Worst | 4 | $500 | $2,000 | $1,850 | $150 |
| Expected | 6 | $625 | $3,750 | $1,850 | $1,900 |
| Best | 8 | $750 | $6,000 | $1,850 | $4,150 |

### 12-month run rate (Tier 1-2 mix at maturity)

- 2 campaigns/month average (15-20 mins drive between Tri-Valley and South Bay)
- $3,500 average net per campaign
- **~$84K annual net** before owner draws / taxes
- Doubling cadence (1 campaign/wk) = ~$168K annual net

### Costs per campaign (detail)

| Line item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Postcard print, 6,000 pieces, 8.5x11 14pt | ~$330 |
| EDDM postage @ $0.247 x 6,000 | $1,482 |
| EDDM facing slips / bundling | $0 (DIY) |
| Misc (driving to DDU, design tweaks, mailing supplies) | ~$80 |
| **Total per campaign** | **~$1,892** |

### Break-even

At Tier 1-2 average pricing (~$650/slot), **3 sold slots covers all costs**.
4+ sold = profit. Target 6-8 sold for full economics.

## 8. Risk & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low fill rate on first campaign | Start with Tier 2 pricing on first campaign; sell aggressively in person; commit to running campaign at 4-slot floor. |
| Print/proof errors | Require written proof sign-off from every advertiser before submitting print order. |
| EDDM route closed / DDU rejection | Validate routes 2 weeks pre-drop at eddm.usps.com; call destination DDU to confirm drop window. |
| Competing-category complaint | Service agreement explicitly defines category exclusivity scope; no refunds after proof sign-off. |
| Personal liability | Bind general liability insurance day 1; convert to LLC at $50K trailing revenue. |

## 9. Success Metrics

- **Sales conversion:** % of cold outreaches -> meetings -> closed slots.
  Target 15-20% close rate on meetings booked.
- **Fill rate:** slots sold / slots available per campaign. Target 75%+ by
  campaign 3.
- **Renewal rate:** % of campaign-1 advertisers who buy campaign 2. Target 50%+.
- **CAC -> LTV:** track total hours-to-close per advertiser; aim for <3 hours
  of selling per slot at maturity.
- **Net margin per campaign:** Target 50%+ net.

## 10. Glossary (CA-specific)

- **FBN** — Fictitious Business Name. CA's term for a DBA.
- **CDTFA** — California Department of Tax and Fee Administration. Sales/use tax authority.
- **FTB** — California Franchise Tax Board. Income/franchise tax authority.
- **DDU** — Destination Delivery Unit. The post office that delivers your zip code.
- **EDDM Retail** — DIY EDDM (≤5,000 pieces/day per zip, drop at DDU). What you'll use.
- **EDDM BMEU** — Business Mail Entry Unit (BMEU) drop, requires permit. Skip until volume grows.
