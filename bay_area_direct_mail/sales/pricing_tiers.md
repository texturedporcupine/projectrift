# SF Bay Area Pricing Tiers

> Single source of truth for slot pricing across community tiers. Update this
> file before discounting; downstream materials reference these numbers.

| Tier | Communities | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Danville, Los Gatos, Saratoga, Los Altos / Los Altos Hills, Palo Alto (south), Piedmont | **$700-900** | **$1,100-1,400** |
| **Tier 2** | San Ramon, Pleasanton, Dublin | **$550-750** | **$850-1,100** |
| **Tier 3** | Fremont (Warm Springs), Livermore, Brentwood, Rocklin/Roseville | **$400-600** | **$650-850** |

Bay Area pricing is **20-40% above Austin** to reflect higher local business
revenue, higher cost-of-living expectations, and higher CAC norms in the
Tri-Valley and South Bay markets.

## Discount policy (defaults)

- **Multi-campaign:** -15% on slot 2 if booked at signing for slot 1.
- **3-campaign annual partner:** -10% all slots, locked rate, ROFR on
  category exclusivity.
- **Premium placement:** ~+50-60% over standard for back-panel anchor
  + first-page mention.
- **No agency / referral discount in year 1** — protect margin.

## Floor (do not go below)

| Tier | Standard floor | Premium floor |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | $650 | $1,000 |
| Tier 2 | $500 | $800 |
| Tier 3 | $375 | $600 |

If a deal requires going below the floor, **reduce slot count instead**
(e.g., 5-slot campaign at full price beats a 7-slot campaign at floor) so
unit economics are preserved.
