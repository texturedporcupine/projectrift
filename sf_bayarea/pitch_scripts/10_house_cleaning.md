# Pitch Script — House Cleaning

**Category:** House Cleaning / Maid Service
**Target:** Owner or GM of local house cleaning company

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. I work with home service businesses in [COMMUNITY] to reach every household in the neighborhood. I have an exclusive cleaning-service slot on an upcoming postcard campaign. Quick question — are you looking to grow your residential client base?"

## The Pitch

> "I'm putting together a shared postcard going to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes in [COMMUNITY] through USPS EDDM. It features 6–8 local businesses, and **only one house cleaning company gets on the card.**
>
> "[COMMUNITY] is a community of dual-income professional households with [MEDIAN_HHI] median income. These are people who value their time more than the cost of cleaning — they'd rather pay $150–300 for a professional clean than spend their Saturday doing it themselves.
>
> "Your ad space is [STANDARD_PRICE]. If your average recurring client pays $200/visit biweekly — that's $5,200/year in revenue. Pick up 3–5 new recurring clients from this campaign and you've generated $15,000–26,000 in annual revenue from a [STANDARD_PRICE] investment."

## Key Selling Points

- House cleaning is recurring revenue — biweekly or monthly clients pay for years
- Affluent Bay Area households outsource cleaning as a quality-of-life investment
- New-client offers (first clean discounted) drive strong postcard response
- Direct mail reaches all household decision-makers, not just the one who Googles
- Category exclusivity means no competing cleaning companies on the card

## Objection Handling

**"We get clients from Yelp and Google."**
> "Online platforms are competitive and review-driven. Direct mail reaches people who haven't started searching yet — they see your offer, realize they need it, and call. You're creating demand, not competing for it."

**"We have a hard time with staffing, not leads."**
> "Understood. But with a steady lead flow, you can be selective about which clients you take — higher-value homes, better neighborhoods, cleaner routing. This helps you upgrade your client base, not just grow it."

**"Cleaning is a low-margin business — I can't afford advertising."**
> "Let's look at the math: [STANDARD_PRICE] to reach [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes. One new biweekly client at $200/clean is $5,200/year. Your cost to acquire that client is [STANDARD_PRICE]. That's a 7–10x return in year one alone."

## Close

> "The house cleaning slot is open for [COMMUNITY]. 50% deposit — [DEPOSIT_AMOUNT] — holds it through [HOLD_DATE]. I can send a sample layout today. Ready to move forward?"

- **Pleasanton:** "Pleasanton families with kids and busy schedules are ideal recurring cleaning clients. They want reliable, trustworthy service."
