# Pitch Script — General Dentist

**Category:** General Dentistry
**Target:** Practice owner, office manager, or marketing coordinator

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. I help dental practices in [COMMUNITY] reach every household in the neighborhood — and I have a dentistry-exclusive opportunity on an upcoming direct mail campaign. Do you have two minutes?"

## The Pitch

> "I'm putting together a shared postcard going to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] households in [COMMUNITY]. It's a professional, full-color 8.5×11 mailer with 6–8 local businesses — and **only one general dentist gets on the card.**
>
> "What that means for your practice: every homeowner in [COMMUNITY] sees your name, your new-patient offer, and your phone number — and no other dental practice is competing for their attention on the same piece.
>
> "[COMMUNITY] is a community of [HOUSEHOLDS] households with [MEDIAN_HHI] median income and [HOMEOWNERSHIP] homeownership. These are families who invest in healthcare and expect quality. A strong new-patient offer on this card — maybe a free cleaning and exam for new patients, or a whitening special — could bring in 10–20 new patient inquiries.
>
> "Your investment is [STANDARD_PRICE]. The lifetime value of a dental patient is $1,000–2,000+/year in hygiene and treatment. You need ONE new family from this campaign to see a positive return for years."

## Key Selling Points

- Dental patient lifetime value ($1K–2K+/year) makes ROI math extremely favorable
- Families are always looking for a dentist when they move or are dissatisfied with current provider
- Exclusive category — no competing dentists on the same card
- Affluent communities value quality dental care and are willing to pay out-of-pocket
- New patient offers (free cleaning, whitening) drive high response rates on postcards

## Objection Handling

**"We're already at capacity."**
> "That's great — you're in demand. But are all your hygiene slots filled? Most practices have mid-week or early-morning openings they'd love to fill. Even 5–10 new patients fills those gaps and increases production."

**"We rely on insurance networks for patients."**
> "Insurance referrals bring patients in, but direct mail brings in patients who choose you — not just because you're on their plan, but because they like your offer. Those patients tend to be more loyal and more likely to accept treatment."

**"Our patients come from word of mouth."**
> "Word of mouth is powerful, but it's unpredictable. Direct mail is word of mouth at scale — you're introducing yourself to every household in the neighborhood at once. When a neighbor mentions your name and they already saw your postcard, the referral converts faster."

## Close

> "I can hold the general dentistry slot for you through [HOLD_DATE]. A 50% deposit — [DEPOSIT_AMOUNT] — locks it in. We'll design your ad space, you approve the proof, and then [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] households see your practice. Ready to go?"
