# Pitch Script — Med Spa / Aesthetics

**Category:** Med Spa / Aesthetics
**Target:** Owner, medical director, or practice manager

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. I'm organizing a premium direct mail campaign for [COMMUNITY], and I have the aesthetics/med spa slot open — exclusive to one practice. Interested?"

## The Pitch

> "This is a shared postcard going to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes in [COMMUNITY]. It's full-color, professionally designed, and mailed via USPS EDDM. There are 6–8 businesses on the card — and **only one med spa or aesthetics practice gets the spot.**
>
> "[COMMUNITY] is exactly the demographic that drives med spa revenue — [MEDIAN_HHI] median income, high disposable income, and a population that invests in appearance and wellness. Think Botox, fillers, laser treatments, body contouring. Your typical new patient books a $500–2,000 first treatment and comes back 3–4 times per year.
>
> "Your investment is [STANDARD_PRICE] for standard or [PREMIUM_PRICE] for premium placement. One new patient from this card could generate $2,000–8,000 in annual revenue — and the lifetime value is even higher."

## Key Selling Points

- Med spa patients have high lifetime value ($5K–20K+)
- Affluent Bay Area demographics match med spa target audience perfectly
- New patient offers (free consultation, intro Botox pricing) drive strong response
- Premium direct mail matches the premium brand positioning of aesthetics practices
- Category exclusivity eliminates competing practices on the same piece

## Objection Handling

**"Our patients come from Instagram/social media."**
> "Social media is great for awareness, but direct mail reaches people who aren't on Instagram — and there are a lot of them in [COMMUNITY]. Plus, when someone sees your social post AND gets your postcard, conversion rates multiply."

**"We're a premium brand — direct mail feels mass-market."**
> "This isn't a coupon flyer. It's a professionally designed, full-color 14pt cardstock piece. The production quality matches your brand. And the exclusivity — only one med spa on the card — reinforces your premium positioning."

## Close

> "The med spa slot for [COMMUNITY] is open. [STANDARD_PRICE] for standard, [PREMIUM_PRICE] for premium placement with a larger ad space. 50% deposit holds it. Want to see a sample layout?"

---

## Bay Area Local Hooks

- **Danville:** "Danville and Blackhawk residents are your ideal demo — affluent, appearance-conscious, and willing to pay for quality aesthetic treatments."
- **Los Gatos:** "Los Gatos has a boutique, wellness-oriented culture. Med spa services fit naturally alongside yoga studios and organic markets."
- **Saratoga:** "Saratoga's ultra-affluent population is underserved for aesthetics — most drive to Los Gatos or Palo Alto. A strong local presence here is a competitive advantage."
- **Palo Alto:** "Tech executive households in south Palo Alto have the income and the motivation for aesthetic treatments. Appearance matters in Silicon Valley."
