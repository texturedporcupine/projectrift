# Pitch Script — Landscaping

**Category:** Landscaping / Lawn Care
**Target:** Owner or GM of local landscaping company

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. I help home service businesses in [COMMUNITY] reach every homeowner in the neighborhood. I have a landscaping-exclusive advertising opportunity — are you interested in getting your business in front of [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homeowners?"

## The Pitch

> "I'm putting together a shared direct mail postcard going to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes in [COMMUNITY]. The card features 6–8 local businesses, and **only one landscaping company gets the spot.**
>
> "[COMMUNITY] homeowners are serious about curb appeal. With [HOMEOWNERSHIP] homeownership, active HOAs enforcing appearance standards, and median home values of [MEDIAN_HOME_VALUE], these people invest in their yards. Many are also transitioning to drought-tolerant landscaping — which means they need a professional who understands California water regulations and native plants.
>
> "Your ad space is [STANDARD_PRICE]. An average landscaping project runs $3,000–10,000+ for design/install, and monthly maintenance accounts are $200–500/month. One new design project or 2–3 maintenance clients covers your investment multiple times over."

## Key Selling Points

- HOA communities require maintained yards — built-in demand for professional landscaping
- California drought-tolerant landscaping transition is driving new project demand
- Recurring monthly maintenance revenue from each new client
- High-value homeowners prefer established, professional landscaping companies
- Exclusivity eliminates competitor advertising on the same mailer

## Objection Handling

**"I'm already booked through the season."**
> "That's a great problem. But you know as well as I do that spring bookings don't guarantee fall retention. Building your pipeline now means you start next season with a full roster instead of scrambling."

**"My crews are all from referrals."**
> "Referrals are your best source — this amplifies them. When someone hears about you from a neighbor and they've already seen your name on a postcard, that's a double-touch. Your close rate on referrals goes up."

## Close

> "I've got the landscaping slot open for [COMMUNITY]. Want me to hold it until [HOLD_DATE]? 50% deposit to lock in. I'll send you a sample layout today."

---

## Bay Area Local Hooks

- **Danville:** "Homes along Green Valley Road and in the foothills have large lots and mature landscapes — these homeowners spend $5K–20K on annual landscape maintenance."
- **San Ramon:** "HOA standards in Dougherty Valley and Windemere require well-maintained yards. Homeowners need a reliable landscape partner."
- **Saratoga:** "One-acre minimum lots with mature oaks and gardens. Premium landscape maintenance budgets of $1K+/month are common."
- **Los Altos Hills:** "Estate-scale properties with extensive grounds. A single new client here could be worth $15K–25K+ annually in maintenance."
