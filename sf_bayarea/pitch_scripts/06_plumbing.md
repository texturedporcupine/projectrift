# Pitch Script — Plumbing

**Category:** Plumbing
**Target:** Owner or GM of local plumbing company

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. I'm putting together a direct mail campaign for [COMMUNITY] and I have the plumbing slot open — exclusive to one company. Interested in hearing more?"

## The Pitch

> "Here's the setup: a professionally designed 8.5×11 postcard goes to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes in [COMMUNITY] via USPS Every Door Direct Mail. It features 6–8 local businesses, and **only one plumber gets on the card.**
>
> "[COMMUNITY] has a mix of [HOUSING_MIX] — that means slab leaks, repiping needs, water heater replacements, and general plumbing repairs are a constant. These are [MEDIAN_HHI]-income households that call a professional rather than trying YouTube repairs.
>
> "Your ad space is [STANDARD_PRICE]. With an average plumbing service call at $300–500 and water heater replacements at $2,000–4,000, the math works fast. One water heater replacement covers your investment several times over."

## Key Selling Points

- Plumbing is an emergency service — top-of-mind awareness wins
- Older Bay Area housing stock drives consistent repair demand
- High average ticket ($300–500 service, $2K–4K+ water heater/repipe)
- Homeowners don't shop around during emergencies — they call who they know
- Being on the fridge (via postcard) when the pipe bursts = phone rings

## Objection Handling

**"We get enough business from Google/Yelp."**
> "Google and Yelp are great, but everyone's competing there. Direct mail reaches people BEFORE they have an emergency. When the pipe bursts at 9pm, they grab the card off the fridge — not their laptop."

**"Plumbing is an emergency business — people search when they need us."**
> "Exactly. And direct mail is the fridge magnet strategy — your name is already in the house when the emergency happens. The homeowner who saw your postcard last week is 5x more likely to call you than search Google."

## Close

> "The plumbing slot for [COMMUNITY] is open. I can hold it through [HOLD_DATE]. 50% deposit — [DEPOSIT_AMOUNT] — and I handle everything from design to EDDM delivery. Want to lock it in?"

---

## Bay Area Local Hooks

- **Danville:** "Core Danville homes from the 1970s–80s have original copper and cast iron plumbing — repipe season is here."
- **Pleasanton:** "Older neighborhoods like Birdland and Val Vista have 40+ year old plumbing systems. These homeowners need a trusted plumber on speed dial."
- **Los Gatos:** "Hillside homes in Los Gatos face unique plumbing challenges — gravity-fed systems, older pipes, and tree root intrusion."
- **Livermore:** "Livermore's housing stock ranges from 1960s ranch homes to 2010s new construction — every vintage has plumbing needs."
