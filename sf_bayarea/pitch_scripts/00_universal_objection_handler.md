# Universal Objection Handler

*Works across all business categories. These responses are market-neutral and apply to any Bay Area community.*

---

## "We don't do direct mail."

> "I hear that a lot — and I get it. Most direct mail is junk. Here's why this is different: it's not a solo flyer that goes straight to recycling. It's a shared postcard with 6–8 local businesses that homeowners actually keep because there are multiple offers they care about. Think of it like a mini community coupon book — but on a single professional card. And with category exclusivity, your competitor can't be on it."

## "We're too busy right now."

> "That's actually the best time to advertise — you're busy, which means you're good at what you do. But here's the risk: business cycles. Every home service company has a slow season. The campaigns you run when you're busy are what fill your pipeline when things slow down.
>
> Plus, if you're at capacity, this lets you be selective — take only the highest-value jobs and pass on the small stuff. That's how you grow margins, not just revenue."

## "I already advertise on Google/Yelp/social media."

> "Those channels are great — and this complements them, not competes with them. Here's why:
>
> Google and Yelp reach people who are already searching for your service. Direct mail reaches people who AREN'T searching yet — but will be. It plants your name before the need arises.
>
> When a homeowner sees your postcard, files it away, and then their AC breaks down three weeks later — guess whose number they call? Not the one they'd have to Google. Yours. It's already on the counter.
>
> The strongest advertising strategies use multiple channels. This adds a channel you're probably not using."

## "How do I track results?"

> "Great question — here are three proven methods:
>
> 1. **Dedicated phone number.** We can include a tracking number on your ad so every call from the postcard is measured separately. Many providers offer this for $10–20/month.
>
> 2. **Unique offer code.** Put a special promo code or mention code on your ad — 'Mention DANVILLE10 for 10% off.' Every customer who uses it came from the card.
>
> 3. **'How did you hear about us?'** The simplest method. Train your front desk to ask every new caller. You'd be surprised how many say 'I got your postcard.'
>
> I'll also check in with you 2 weeks after delivery to see what kind of response you're getting."
