# Pitch Script — Roofing

**Category:** Roofing
**Target:** Owner or GM of local roofing company

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. I help home service businesses reach every household in [COMMUNITY] — and I have an exclusive roofing slot on an upcoming direct mail campaign. Quick question: are you looking to grow your residential book in [COMMUNITY]?"

## The Pitch

> "I'm organizing a shared postcard going to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes in [COMMUNITY] via USPS EDDM. There are 6–8 businesses on the card, and **only one roofing company gets the spot.**
>
> "[COMMUNITY] has homes with [ROOF_AGE] — many approaching their 20–30 year replacement window. At [MEDIAN_HHI] median income and [MEDIAN_HOME_VALUE] home values, these homeowners invest in quality roofing. An average residential roof replacement in the Bay Area runs $15,000–30,000+.
>
> "Your ad space is [STANDARD_PRICE]. One roof replacement from this campaign could return 20–40x your advertising investment. Even a few repair calls cover the cost."

## Key Selling Points

- Highest average ticket of any home service category ($15K–30K+ per job)
- One closed deal from the campaign = massive ROI
- Bay Area homeowners with older homes need roof assessment/replacement
- Roofing is a trust business — direct mail builds local credibility
- Category exclusivity = no competing roofer on the same card

## Objection Handling

**"We get all our work from storm chasers and insurance."**
> "Insurance work is volume-dependent on weather events. Direct mail gives you a steady pipeline of homeowner-initiated projects — remodels, proactive replacements, solar prep. That's higher-margin work that doesn't depend on weather."

**"Roofing is a big-ticket decision — people don't buy from a postcard."**
> "You're right — they don't buy from a postcard. But they DO call from a postcard. The postcard gets your phone to ring. Your sales process closes the deal. Direct mail is the top of the funnel."

## Close

> "The roofing category for [COMMUNITY] is still open. I can hold it through [HOLD_DATE]. 50% deposit locks it. One roof from this campaign pays for the ad space many times over. Want to move forward?"

---

## Bay Area Local Hooks

- **Danville:** "Homes in the foothills take intense sun exposure — roofs age faster at elevation. 1980s–90s builds are in their replacement window."
- **Brentwood:** "Brentwood has some of the highest sustained temperatures in the Bay Area. UV and heat damage shorten roof life significantly."
- **Livermore:** "Hot summers and aging housing stock — Livermore is prime territory for residential roof replacements."
- **Pleasanton:** "Vintage Hills and older Pleasanton neighborhoods have 30–40 year old composition roofs reaching end of life."
