# Pitch Script — Pool Service

**Category:** Pool Service / Maintenance
**Target:** Owner or GM of local pool service company

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. With pool season ramping up in the Tri-Valley, I have a pool-service-exclusive advertising opportunity that puts your business in front of every homeowner in [COMMUNITY]. Got a minute?"

## The Pitch

> "I'm organizing a shared postcard going to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes in [COMMUNITY] via USPS Every Door Direct Mail. It features 6–8 local businesses — and **only one pool service company gets on the card.**
>
> "[COMMUNITY] has a high concentration of homes with pools — especially in [POOL_NEIGHBORHOODS]. These are [MEDIAN_HHI]-income households that outsource pool maintenance rather than DIY. With Tri-Valley summer temps hitting triple digits, pool season runs April through October here.
>
> "Your investment is [STANDARD_PRICE]. A standard monthly pool service plan runs $150–250/month. Pick up just 3–5 new monthly clients and you've covered your ad cost for the year — with recurring revenue."

## Key Selling Points

- Pool service is recurring monthly revenue — each new client pays for years
- Bay Area Tri-Valley communities have high pool density
- Seasonal timing (spring pre-season) maximizes response
- Affluent homeowners outsource pool care — they don't DIY
- One ad spot per category = no competitor on the card

## Objection Handling

**"Pool season is already my busy time."**
> "Exactly — you're busy now, but what about October through March? A postcard that goes out in spring builds your pipeline. Plus, clients you acquire now stay on your monthly plan year-round."

**"I'm a one-person operation, I can't take on more clients."**
> "Fair point. But if you're at capacity, you have pricing power. This card could help you upgrade to higher-margin clients in premium neighborhoods — replace your lowest-paying accounts with [COMMUNITY] homeowners willing to pay a premium."

## Close

> "Pool season is here. I can hold the slot until [HOLD_DATE] — after that, I'll offer it to [COMPETITOR_MENTION]. 50% deposit locks you in. Want me to send the community data?"

---

## Bay Area Local Hooks

- **Danville:** "Blackhawk has some of the highest pool density in Contra Costa County — a single route through that community is a goldmine for pool service."
- **San Ramon:** "Canyon Lakes community was literally built around lakes and water features — pool ownership here is well above average."
- **Pleasanton:** "Ruby Hill estates almost universally have pools. Premium clientele who want white-glove pool service."
- **Brentwood:** "Newer Brentwood communities like Summerset and Shadow Lakes — tons of new pools that need their first service provider."
