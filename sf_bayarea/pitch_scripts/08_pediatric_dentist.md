# Pitch Script — Pediatric Dentist

**Category:** Pediatric Dentistry
**Target:** Practice owner, office manager

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. I work with family-oriented businesses in [COMMUNITY], and I have a pediatric-dentistry-exclusive opportunity on an upcoming direct mail campaign. Do you have a minute?"

## The Pitch

> "I'm putting together a shared postcard going to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes in [COMMUNITY]. It features 6–8 local businesses, and **only one pediatric dentist gets on the card.**
>
> "[COMMUNITY] is one of the most family-dense communities in the Bay Area — [FAMILY_STAT]. Parents here are actively looking for pediatric specialists, and they trust recommendations that come through community channels.
>
> "A new pediatric dental patient family typically has 2–3 children, each needing biannual visits at $200–400 per visit. One new family could represent $1,500–3,000+ in annual production — and they stay with you for 10+ years as their kids grow.
>
> "Your ad space is [STANDARD_PRICE]. The lifetime value of one new family makes this a no-brainer investment."

## Key Selling Points

- Pediatric patients stay for 10+ years through childhood
- One new family = 2–3 patients with biannual visits
- Family-dense Bay Area communities are ideal demographic
- Parents trust community-oriented advertising over Google ads
- Exclusive category — no competing pediatric practices

## Objection Handling

**"We already have a waitlist."**
> "A waitlist means demand exceeds supply — which means you have pricing power. Use this campaign to attract the highest-value families, and convert your waitlist into a competitive advantage."

**"Parents find us through their regular dentist."**
> "Referrals from GPs are great, but they're dependent on the referring dentist's relationship. Direct mail creates your own referral channel — parents see your name, remember it, and call when their pediatrician or a friend mentions needing a kids' dentist."

## Close

> "The pediatric dentistry slot is available for [COMMUNITY]. 50% deposit — [DEPOSIT_AMOUNT] — holds it through [HOLD_DATE]. One new family covers the cost for years. Shall I send the details?"
