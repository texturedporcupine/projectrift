# Pitch Script — HVAC

**Category:** HVAC / Heating & Cooling
**Target:** Owner or GM of local HVAC company serving Bay Area suburbs

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. I work with home service businesses in [COMMUNITY] to get them in front of every household in the neighborhood — and I have an HVAC-exclusive opportunity I wanted to run by you."

## The Pitch

> "I'm putting together a shared postcard mailer that goes to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes in [COMMUNITY] through USPS Every Door Direct Mail. It's a full-color 8.5×11 postcard featuring 6–8 local businesses — and here's the key part: **only one HVAC company gets on the card.**
>
> "So if you're the HVAC company on this card, your competitors aren't. Every homeowner in [COMMUNITY] sees your name, your offer, and your phone number — and nobody else in your category.
>
> "[COMMUNITY] has [HOUSEHOLDS] households with a median income of [MEDIAN_HHI]. These are homeowners with [HOUSING_AGE] — many with systems that are 10–15 years old and due for replacement or major service. With Tri-Valley summers hitting 100°+, HVAC isn't optional — it's survival.
>
> "Your investment is [STANDARD_PRICE] for a standard ad space. That works out to about $0.10–0.15 per household reached. If your average service call is $300 and your average system install is $8,000–15,000, you'd only need one or two calls from this campaign to see a strong return."

## Key Selling Points

- Tri-Valley heat makes HVAC a year-round necessity — not seasonal
- Average HVAC job value ($300 service, $8K–15K install) makes ROI math compelling
- Homeowners in [COMMUNITY] have older systems that need maintenance/replacement
- Exclusivity means no competing HVAC ads on the same piece of mail
- Direct mail has 4.4% response rate vs. 0.12% for email (DMA)

## Objection Handling

**"We get all our business from referrals."**
> "Referrals are great — and this amplifies them. When a neighbor sees your name on the postcard and then hears about you from a friend, that's two touchpoints. Referrals work even better when people already recognize your brand."

**"We tried direct mail and it didn't work."**
> "Most direct mail that fails is a solo mailer — one business, one card. This is a shared postcard with 6–8 businesses. Recipients keep it because there are multiple offers they're interested in. Your HVAC ad stays on the counter next to the dentist and the pool guy."

**"That's too expensive."**
> "Let me flip that: at [STANDARD_PRICE], you're reaching [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes for about a dime each. Your average install is $10K+. Even if just one homeowner calls you from this card, that's a 10x return on your ad spend."

**"I need to think about it."**
> "Totally understand. Here's the thing — I can only have one HVAC company on this card. I've got [OTHER_INTEREST] also looking at the category. I can hold it for you through [HOLD_DATE], but after that I'll need to move on. Want me to send you the community data and a sample layout?"

## Close

> "I can hold the HVAC category for you through [HOLD_DATE]. To lock it in, it's a 50% deposit — [DEPOSIT_AMOUNT] — and we handle everything from there: design, proofing, printing, and EDDM delivery. Sound like a plan?"

## Bay Area Local Hooks

- **Danville:** "With homes in Blackhawk and near Diablo Country Club, you're reaching households that don't blink at a $15K system replacement."
- **San Ramon:** "Dougherty Valley has thousands of homes built in the 2000s–2010s — those original HVAC systems are hitting their 15-year service window right now."
- **Pleasanton:** "Ruby Hill and Vintage Hills are full of larger homes with multi-zone systems. These homeowners need a reliable HVAC partner."
- **Dublin:** "The new developments in Boulevard and Positano have warranties expiring — those homeowners are about to start shopping for HVAC service providers."
