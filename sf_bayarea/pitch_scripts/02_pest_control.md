# Pitch Script — Pest Control

**Category:** Pest Control
**Target:** Owner or branch manager of local pest control company

---

## Opening Hook

> "Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME]. Quick question — are you actively trying to grow your residential customer base in [COMMUNITY]? I have a pest-control-exclusive advertising opportunity that puts your name in front of every homeowner in the neighborhood."

## The Pitch

> "I'm organizing a shared direct mail postcard going to [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes in [COMMUNITY] via USPS Every Door Direct Mail. The card features 6–8 local service businesses, and **the pest control slot is exclusive to one company.**
>
> "[COMMUNITY] homeowners deal with [LOCAL_PEST_ISSUE] — and they're actively looking for reliable providers. These are [MEDIAN_HHI] household-income neighborhoods with [HOMEOWNERSHIP] homeownership rates. Homeowners who own their property invest in protection.
>
> "Your ad space is [STANDARD_PRICE]. That gets you in front of [ROUTE_HOUSEHOLDS] homes for about a dime each. If your average annual pest control plan is $400–600 and you pick up even 5–10 new customers from this campaign, that's $2,000–6,000 in recurring annual revenue."

## Key Selling Points

- Pest control is a recurring revenue business — one new customer = years of service revenue
- Bay Area homes adjacent to open space/hillsides have significant pest pressure
- Homeowners prefer a single trusted provider — direct mail builds that initial trust
- Exclusivity means the competition can't be on the same card
- Seasonal timing (spring/summer) aligns with peak pest awareness

## Objection Handling

**"We already do door-to-door."**
> "Door-to-door is effective but labor-intensive. This card hits every door on the route simultaneously for $0.10/household. It's the air cover for your ground game — when your technician knocks, they already recognize the name from the postcard."

**"Our customers are all on annual plans — we don't need new leads."**
> "Annual plan customers churn at 15–20% industry average. This keeps your pipeline full so you're backfilling churn without thinking about it. Plus, new homeowners move into [COMMUNITY] every month who don't have a provider yet."

**"We focus on commercial, not residential."**
> "Understood. But many commercial pest control companies I work with also have a residential division that could use the volume. Even if it's 20% of your business, a few thousand dollars in cost-effective leads is pure margin."

## Close

> "I can hold the pest control category for you through [HOLD_DATE]. It's a 50% deposit to lock in — [DEPOSIT_AMOUNT]. I handle the design, you just give me your logo, best offer, and phone number. Deal?"

## Bay Area Local Hooks

- **Danville:** "Properties backing up to Mt. Diablo foothills see rodent, ant, and spider pressure year-round. Homeowners here need a provider they trust."
- **San Ramon:** "The newer developments in Dougherty Valley are right against the hillsides — ground squirrels, ants, and spiders are a constant issue."
- **Dublin:** "New construction in Dublin means disturbed soil and pest migration — new homeowners are actively looking for their first pest control provider."
- **Brentwood:** "Agricultural adjacency in East Contra Costa means serious pest pressure — rodents, ants, and termites are part of life out here."
