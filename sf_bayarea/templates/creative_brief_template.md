## EDDM Requirements

**Address Side (Back):**
- EDDM indicia in upper-right corner (provided by USPS — download from eddm.usps.com)
- Indicia must read: "ECRWSS" / "EDDM" / "Postal Customer" / "[City, State ZIP]"
- Leave 4.5" × 2.5" clear area in lower-right for USPS delivery address block
- "ECRWSS" must appear in the address area
- "Postal Customer" as the recipient name

**Front:**
- No EDDM-specific requirements — use for ad space layout

## Design Notes

- **Overall feel:** Professional, clean, trustworthy. Not cluttered or coupon-book style.
- **Color palette:** Use a cohesive palette across the card. Individual ad spaces should incorporate each advertiser's brand colors, but the overall card should feel unified.
- **Typography:** Clear, readable sans-serif for body text. Allow advertiser brand fonts in their ad spaces.
- **Hierarchy:** Each ad space should prominently feature: (1) Business name/logo, (2) Offer/CTA, (3) Phone number. Website is secondary.
- **Premium ad spaces** get ~50% more area than standard spaces and preferred positioning (front, upper).
- **No stock photos** unless approved by advertiser. Logos and text-based designs preferred for clean reproduction.
