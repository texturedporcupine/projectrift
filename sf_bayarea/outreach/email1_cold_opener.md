**Subject Line Options** (pick one):
- Your neighbors are already your customers — here's proof

- One postcard. [X,000] households. Your ad — nobody else in [CATEGORY].

**To:** [BUSINESS OWNER NAME]

**Subject:** [CATEGORY] exclusive: reach every home in [COMMUNITY]
