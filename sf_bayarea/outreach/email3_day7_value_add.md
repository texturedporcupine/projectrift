# Email 3 — Day 7 Value-Add

**Send:** 7 days after Email 1 (4 days after Email 2, if no reply)

**Subject Line Options** (pick one):
- Last note — then I'll leave you alone
- Quick data for [FIRST NAME] — [COMMUNITY] household stats
- Closing [CATEGORY] this week — wanted you to have first shot

---

**To:** [BUSINESS OWNER NAME]
**From:** [YOUR NAME]
**Subject:** Last note — then I'll leave you alone

---

Hi [FIRST NAME],

This is my last email on this — I respect your time.

I wanted to leave you with something useful whether or not we work together. I pulled some data on the [COMMUNITY] market that might help with your own marketing planning:

**[COMMUNITY] ([ZIP]) Snapshot:**
- [HOUSEHOLDS] households, [HOMEOWNERSHIP] homeownership
- Median household income: [MEDIAN_HHI]
- Median home value: [MEDIAN_HOME_VALUE]
- [COMMUNITY_INSIGHT]

That's a dense pocket of homeowners who actively invest in their properties. Whether it's through this postcard or your own outreach, [COMMUNITY] is a market worth targeting.

If the timing isn't right for this campaign, no worries at all. I run these quarterly, and I'd be happy to hold [CATEGORY] for you on the next round — typically at a [DISCOUNT]% early-commitment discount.

Either way, I'll close out the [CATEGORY] slot by [DEADLINE] so the other businesses on the card know we're locked. If you want it, just reply "I'm in" and I'll send the details.

Thanks for your time, [FIRST NAME]. Hope to work together down the road.

Best,
[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR PHONE]

P.S. — If you know another [CATEGORY_ADJACENT] business owner who might be interested, I'd appreciate the referral. Happy to extend a $50 referral credit toward their first campaign.

---

## Personalization Variables

| Variable | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| `[MEDIAN_HOME_VALUE]` | Median home value | $1.8M+ |
| `[COMMUNITY_INSIGHT]` | Local color/fact | Blackhawk and Diablo Country Club communities anchor the high end — these residents spend on home services |
| `[DISCOUNT]` | Early-commitment discount % | 15 |
| `[DEADLINE]` | Final deadline for category | Friday, May 22 |
| `[CATEGORY_ADJACENT]` | Related but non-competing category | plumbing or roofing |

*All other variables same as Email 1.*

| Day | Action | Email |
|-----|--------|-------|
