## 1. Services

Service Provider agrees to provide the following services to Advertiser:

**a) Ad Space.** One (1) exclusive ad space in the Advertiser's business category ([CATEGORY]) on a shared direct mail postcard ("Postcard") to be distributed via USPS Every Door Direct Mail (EDDM) to residential households in [COMMUNITY], California ([ZIP CODE]).

**b) Exclusivity.** Only one (1) business in the [CATEGORY] category will appear on the Postcard. Advertiser will be the sole [CATEGORY] advertiser for this campaign.

**c) Design.** Service Provider will design the Advertiser's ad space based on materials provided by Advertiser (logo, headline, offer, phone number, website). A digital proof will be provided for Advertiser's written approval before printing.

**d) Printing.** Service Provider will coordinate professional printing of the Postcard on 14pt (minimum) cardstock in full color.

**e) Distribution.** Service Provider will handle all USPS EDDM logistics including route selection, facing slips, postage payment, and DDU delivery.
