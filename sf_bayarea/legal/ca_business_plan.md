## Section 1: Legal & Tax Setup (California)

### 1.1 Business Structure

**Recommended starting structure: Sole Proprietorship with FBN**

A sole proprietorship is the simplest way to start in California. No state filing is required for the entity itself — you simply begin operating. However, if you operate under any name other than your legal name, you must file a Fictitious Business Name (FBN) statement.

**Alternative: LLC**
- Provides liability protection and professional credibility
- California imposes an $800/year minimum franchise tax on all LLCs regardless of revenue
- First-year franchise tax may be waived if LLC is formed in Q4 (October–December)
- File Articles of Organization with the CA Secretary of State ($70 filing fee)
- After the first year, may also owe an LLC annual fee if gross revenue exceeds $250K

**Recommendation:** Start as sole proprietorship. Convert to LLC once generating consistent revenue (>$3K/month) to justify the $800 annual cost.

### 1.2 FBN (Fictitious Business Name) Filing

California requires an FBN filing when operating under a name other than your personal legal name.

**Process:**
1. Search for name availability (not required but recommended — check Secretary of State website)
2. File FBN statement with your **county recorder** (not county clerk — this is different from Texas)
3. Cost: $26–100 depending on county (Contra Costa: ~$40, Santa Clara: ~$56, Alameda: ~$60)
4. Within 30 days of filing, publish the FBN statement in an **adjudicated newspaper** in your county
5. Publication must run for **4 consecutive weeks**
6. Publication cost: $50–150 depending on newspaper
7. After publication, file the Affidavit of Publication with the county recorder
8. FBN expires after **5 years** — must renew before expiration

**County Recorder Offices:**
- Contra Costa County (Danville, San Ramon, Pleasanton): 651 Pine St, Martinez CA 94553
- Alameda County (Dublin, Fremont, Livermore): 1106 Madison St, Oakland CA 94607
- Santa Clara County (Los Gatos, Saratoga, Los Altos, Palo Alto): 70 W Hedding St, San Jose CA 95110

### 1.3 Business Licenses

California does not have a statewide business license. However, most cities and counties require a local business license. This is typically called a "Business Tax Certificate" or "Business License."

**Typical costs:** $50–200/year depending on city

**How to obtain:**
- Search "[city name] business license" for your city's application
- Most can be completed online
- Annual renewal required

**Key cities and contacts:**
- Danville: Town of Danville Business License, (925) 314-3388

- Pleasanton: City of Pleasanton Business License, (925) 931-5500
- Dublin: City of Dublin Business License, (925) 833-6650

### 1.4 Tax Obligations

**Federal taxes:** Report business income on Schedule C (Form 1040). Estimated quarterly tax payments required if you expect to owe $1,000+ in tax.

**California state income tax:** California taxes all income earned by residents. File Form 540 with the Franchise Tax Board (FTB). Estimated quarterly payments required if you expect to owe $500+ in state tax.

**Self-employment tax:** 15.3% on net self-employment income (12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare). Half is deductible.

**California sales tax considerations:**
- **Advertising services** (selling ad space) are generally NOT subject to CA sales tax
- **Printed materials** (postcards) may be taxable if you are considered the seller of tangible personal property
- The distinction depends on whether you are selling advertising (not taxable) or selling printed products (potentially taxable)
- Consult the CA Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) at cdtfa.ca.gov
- If in doubt, register for a seller's permit (free) and collect/remit sales tax on the printed postcard component

**Estimated quarterly tax payment schedule:**
- April 15 (for Jan–Mar income)
- June 15 (for Apr–May income)
- September 15 (for Jun–Aug income)
- January 15 (for Sep–Dec income)

### 1.5 Insurance

**General liability insurance** is recommended but not legally required for this business type.

- Typical cost: $300–600/year for $1M/$2M general liability
- Covers: property damage, bodily injury, advertising injury claims
- Some commercial printers and business clients may require you to carry GL insurance
- Obtain quotes from: NEXT Insurance, Hiscox, or a local independent agent

### 1.6 Business Banking

Open a separate business checking account to maintain clean financials:

- Chase Business Complete Checking (large branch network in Bay Area)
- Bank of America Business Advantage
- Local option: First Republic Bank (Bay Area focus, high-touch service)
- Online option: Mercury or Relay (no minimum balances, good for startups)

Bring your FBN filing receipt and government-issued ID to open the account.

## Section 5: EDDM Workflow

*Identical to Texas workflow — EDDM is a national program with uniform procedures.*

1. **Route Selection:** Go to eddm.usps.com → enter target zip → filter demographics → select 2–3 carrier routes totaling 5,000–7,000 households
2. **Create Mailing:** Click "Create a Mailing" → enter mailer dimensions → select routes
3. **Generate Documents:** Download facing slips and postage statements for each route
4. **Bundle Postcards:** Bundle by carrier route per USPS specifications. Attach facing slips.
5. **DDU Drop-Off:** Deliver bundled postcards to the Destination Delivery Unit (DDU) post office. Pay $0.247/piece. Get receipt.
6. **Delivery:** USPS delivers within 3–7 business days to every household on selected routes.

## Section 6: Vendor Recommendations (Bay Area)

### Printing
Research commercial printers in the Tri-Valley / East Bay / South Bay area:
- Get quotes for: 5,000–7,000 postcards, 8.5"×11" or 6.5"×9", 14pt cardstock, full color both sides
- Expect: $0.05–0.08/piece for runs of 5,000+
- Ask about: turnaround time, proof process, EDDM bundling service (some printers will bundle for you)

### Online Print Alternatives
- VistaPrint (vistaprint.com) — good for smaller runs, EDDM-compatible sizing available
- GotPrint (gotprint.com) — competitive pricing on postcard stock
- PrintingForLess (printingforless.com) — direct mail specialist, EDDM expertise

### Design
- Canva Pro ($13/month) — good templates for postcard layout
- Fiverr / 99designs — one-time design for $50–200
- Local freelance designer — check Contra Costa / Santa Clara graphic designers
