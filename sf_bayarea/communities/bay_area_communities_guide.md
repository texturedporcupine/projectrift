# SF Bay Area Communities — Direct Mail Target Guide

*Adapted from the Austin TX Communities Direct Mail Guide*
*April 2026*

---

## How to Use This Guide

Each community profile below follows the same structure used in the Austin guide. For every target neighborhood, you'll find:

- **Tier classification** (determines pricing)
- **Demographics** (median household income, home count, homeownership rate)
- **Community character** (what makes it a good EDDM target)
- **EDDM logistics** (zip codes, estimated carrier routes, DDU facility)
- **Recommended ad categories** (which business types are the best fit)
- **Prospecting notes** (where to find advertisers, local commercial corridors)

Before launching a campaign in any community, complete the research checklist at the end of this guide.

## Tier 1 — Premium Communities

### Danville (94526)

**Demographics**
- Median Household Income: $185,000+
- Households: ~18,000
- Homeownership Rate: ~80%
- Median Home Value: $1.8M+

**Community Character**
Danville is the closest Bay Area equivalent to Circle C Ranch in Austin — affluent, suburban, with strong community identity. The town has an active HOA culture, residents who read their mail, and a tight-knit small-town feel despite proximity to the I-680 corridor. The Blackhawk and Diablo Country Club communities anchor the high end. Downtown Danville along Hartz Avenue is the commercial hub with a walkable, village-style main street.

**Why It Works for EDDM**
- Strong HOA infrastructure — multiple active associations that mail regularly to residents
- High homeownership means stable addresses and home-service demand
- Clear geographic boundaries (bounded by Mt. Diablo foothills and I-680)
- Residents are accustomed to receiving and reading community-oriented mail
- Dense local business ecosystem along Hartz Ave, Sycamore Valley Rd, and Camino Ramon

**EDDM Logistics**
- Primary Zip: 94526
- Estimated Carrier Routes: 8-12 qualifying routes
- Target Route Selection: Filter for HHI $100K+, household size 2.5+
- Target 2-3 routes per campaign (5,000-7,000 households)
- DDU Facility: Danville Post Office, 200 Railroad Ave, Danville CA 94526

**Recommended Ad Categories**
| Category | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| HVAC | Large homes, hot Tri-Valley summers, aging systems in established homes |
| Landscaping | Large lots, HOA-maintained appearance standards |
| Pool Service | High pool ownership rate in Blackhawk, Diablo communities |
| General Dentist | Family-oriented community, high dental spend per household |
| Med Spa / Aesthetics | Affluent demographic, multiple med spas already operate locally |
| House Cleaning | Dual-income professional households, large homes |
| Plumbing | Older housing stock in core Danville, new construction in outlying areas |
| Roofing | Mix of 20-40 year old homes needing roof replacement |

**Prospecting Notes**
- Start on Hartz Avenue (downtown Danville) — walk the corridor to identify local service businesses
- Check Danville Area Chamber of Commerce directory for member businesses
- Search Google Maps for "[category] near Danville CA" to build prospect lists
- Blackhawk Business Park and Crow Canyon Commons have service businesses targeting Danville residents
- Local publications: Danville Weekly, Danville Express (advertising leads)

**Demographics**
- Median Household Income: $160,000+
- Households: ~30,000
- Homeownership Rate: ~70%
- Median Home Value: $1.4M+

Heavy tech and corporate presence (Chevron, AT&T, GE Digital) means a population of high-income professionals who value convenience services. The Dougherty Valley master-planned community on the east side is particularly strong for EDDM — newer homes, young families, and consistent block patterns.

- Large master-planned neighborhoods with uniform housing and clear postal routes
- Young professional families with high disposable income
- Bishop Ranch corridor creates a natural commercial hub for advertisers
- Active community events and HOAs (Dougherty Valley, Canyon Lakes, Montevideo)
- Adjacent to Danville — can cross-sell campaigns to advertisers in both markets

- Households: ~10,000

### Los Altos Hills (94022 / 94024)

**Demographics**
- Median Household Income: $300,000+
- Households: ~8,000
- Homeownership Rate: ~90%+
- Median Home Value: $5M+

**Community Character**
The most affluent residential community in the Bay Area. Los Altos Hills is almost entirely single-family residential with 1-acre minimum lot sizes. No commercial development within town limits — all services come from neighboring Los Altos, Palo Alto, and Mountain View. This is ultra-premium territory with the highest household income in the target list.

**Why It Works for EDDM**
- Highest income tier — justifies maximum ad pricing
- Very high homeownership — addresses are stable for years
- Large properties require extensive maintenance services
- Residents are underserved by direct mail — few marketers bother due to lower density
- Clear town boundaries make route selection straightforward

**EDDM Logistics**
- Primary Zips: 94022 (Los Altos proper), 94024 (Los Altos Hills)
- Estimated Carrier Routes: 4-6 qualifying routes (lower density)
- Target 2-3 routes per campaign (3,000-5,000 households — smaller but ultra-premium)
- DDU Facility: Los Altos Post Office, 188 First St, Los Altos CA 94022

**Recommended Ad Categories**
| Category | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Premium Landscaping | 1-acre+ lots, extensive landscape maintenance |
| Concierge Medical / Wellness | High-net-worth individuals value personalized healthcare |
| Luxury Home Renovation | Multi-million dollar homes, high renovation budgets |
| Estate Planning / Wealth Management | Significant assets requiring professional management |
| HVAC (Premium) | Large homes, premium systems |
| Pool Service (Premium) | Pool ownership common in luxury properties |
| Security / Smart Home | High-value properties, security-conscious residents |
| Interior Design | Active renovation and interior upgrade market |

**Prospecting Notes**
- No commercial zone in Los Altos Hills — prospect from neighboring Los Altos downtown (Main St)
- Palo Alto and Mountain View businesses also serve this market
- Look for businesses advertising "serving Los Altos Hills" specifically
- Los Altos Town Crier newspaper for advertiser leads
- Silicon Valley Community Foundation and local charity events for networking with service providers

## Pre-Campaign Research Checklist

Complete this checklist for each community before building the prospect tracker:

- [ ] Go to eddm.usps.com → Enter target zip code
- [ ] Filter: Household income $100K+, Household size 2.5+
- [ ] Screenshot 2-3 viable routes and note household counts
- [ ] Search "[community name] HOA" — confirm active HOA exists
- [ ] Search "[community name] community newsletter" — check for Nextdoor presence, local paper, HOA newsletter
- [ ] Use Google Maps to identify commercial corridors serving the neighborhood
- [ ] Build 30-40 business prospect list across 8-10 non-competing categories
- [ ] Verify DDU post office location and hours
- [ ] Check if community has any direct mail restrictions (rare but possible in some HOA-governed areas)
- [ ] Note any seasonal considerations (e.g., pool season timing, HVAC peak)

## Recommended First Campaign

**Danville (94526)** is the recommended first Bay Area campaign for the following reasons:

1. Strong community identity and HOA infrastructure
2. Residents read their mail (high mail engagement culture)
3. Dense local business ecosystem on Hartz Avenue
4. Clear geographic boundaries for route selection
5. $185K+ median HHI supports Tier 1 pricing ($700-900/ad space)
6. Analogous to Circle C Ranch in Austin — the proven first-campaign template
7. Adjacent to San Ramon for natural second-campaign expansion

**Alternative first campaign:** San Ramon (94582) if you prefer a larger household base and more commercial corridor options.
