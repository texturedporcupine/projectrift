# Category Pitch Scripts — SF Bay Area

Universal objection handling (use for any category): *“We don’t do direct mail”* → EDDM hits **every mailbox** in the route with **category exclusivity** so you are not stacked against another `[Category]`. *“We tried direct mail”* → This is **shared cost** + **fresh creative** + **one winner per category**. *“Too expensive”* → Compare **cost per household** (~$0.25 postage + shared print) to solo mail or high CPM digital in `[Community]`. *“Need to think”* → Category holds **48 hours**; next competitor in your trade gets the slot.

## 1. HVAC

**Hook:** In **`[Community]`** and along **`[Corridor, e.g., Bishop Ranch / Diablo Road]`**, homeowners are **replacing aging systems** and pre-buying maintenance before summer peaks.

**Local angle:** Families in **`[Zip]`** skew **owner-occupied** with **larger homes** — multiple zones, filter programs, and **membership tune-ups** land well here.

**Ask:** We’re filling **one HVAC slot** on a **shared EDDM postcard** to **~[X]k homes** — you’re **not** next to another HVAC shop. **50%** locks the category.
