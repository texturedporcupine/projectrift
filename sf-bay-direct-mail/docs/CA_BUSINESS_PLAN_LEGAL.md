# California Business Plan — Legal & Regulatory (Sections 1.1–1.4)

**Jurisdiction:** California. This section replaces Texas-specific legal content for a sole proprietorship or small LLC operating a shared-postcard EDDM advertising business in the San Francisco Bay Area.

## 1.1 Business structure

- **Sole proprietorship** remains the simplest start: you operate under your legal name or a **Fictitious Business Name (FBN)** (“doing business as”).
- **LLC** adds liability separation but triggers California’s **$800 minimum franchise tax** for most LLCs (Franchise Tax Board), with limited first-year relief if formed in the fourth quarter — verify current FTB rules before forming.

## 1.2 Fictitious Business Name (DBA / FBN)

- File the FBN statement with your **county recorder** (not the county clerk) in the county where you principally operate. Fees are typically **$26–$100** depending on county.
- **Publication:** Within statutory windows, publish the FBN in an **adjudicated newspaper of general circulation** in the county for **four consecutive weeks** (often within 30 days of filing — confirm your county’s instructions). Budget **$50–$150**; retain the **affidavit of publication**.
- **Renewal:** FBNs must be renewed (commonly **every five years** in California) — calendar the renewal.

## 1.3 Tax overview (not tax advice)

- **Sales and use tax:** California taxes many goods and services differently than Texas. **Advertising services** are often not taxable when separately stated, but **printed materials delivered to the client** may be taxable as “printing and related services” in some cases. **Consult the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA)** and a qualified tax professional for your exact fact pattern ([cdtfa.ca.gov](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov)).
- **LLC franchise tax:** If you form an LLC, budget the **$800 minimum** franchise tax unless a specific exemption applies for your entity and year.
- **Federal:** Ordinary income and self-employment tax considerations apply as with any U.S. business; use a CPA for entity choice and estimated payments.

## 1.4 Business licenses and local compliance

- California has **no single statewide general business license** for all businesses.
- **Cities and counties** often require a **local business license** or tax registration; fees commonly **$50–$200+** annually depending on jurisdiction and gross receipts. Apply in the city where you maintain an office or where rules attach to your activity.
- If you hire employees or use independent contractors, follow **EDD** and **IRS** rules for classification, withholding, and reporting.

## 1.5 Bay Area financial assumptions (brief defaults)

- **Ad pricing:** Tier 2 strong markets often **$550–$750** standard placement; premium placement **$850–$1,100** (adjust per community table in `BAY_AREA_COMMUNITIES_GUIDE.md`).
- **Campaign costs (illustrative):** ~$350 printing + ~$1,235+ EDDM postage at **$0.247/pc** for ~5,000 pieces + misc ops — validate with live print quotes and route counts.
