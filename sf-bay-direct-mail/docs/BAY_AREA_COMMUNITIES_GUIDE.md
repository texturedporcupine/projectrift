# Bay Area Communities Guide — Shared Postcard Direct Mail

Reference for tiering, demographics, and recommended advertiser categories. Aligns with the SF Bay Area project brief (April 2026).

## How to Use This Guide

For each community you target: confirm EDDM routes at [eddm.usps.com](https://eddm.usps.com), verify HOA/newsletter signals, map commercial corridors, then build a 30-40 business prospect list across 8-10 non-competing categories.

## Profile: Danville (94526)

- **Tier:** 1 (premium corridor)
- **Households:** 18,000+ in broader area; target 5,000-8,000 per EDDM campaign via 2-3 routes
- **Character:** Established East Bay suburb, strong HOA presence, high homeownership, golf and family-oriented retail corridors along Diablo Road / downtown Danville
- **Why mail works:** Residents accustomed to community mail and HOA communications; clear geographic identity (“Danville” vs. adjacent unincorporated pockets)
- **Recommended categories (first pass):** HVAC, landscaping, pool, general dentistry, med spa, pest control, house cleaning, plumbing

## Profile: San Ramon (94582 / 94583)

- **Tier:** 1-2
- **Households:** 30,000+ citywide; segment Bishop Ranch / Windemere / gated pockets for route selection
- **Character:** Master-planned growth, tech-commuter families, strong retail at San Ramon City Center
- **Commercial corridors:** Bishop Ranch office park perimeter, Crow Canyon, Bollinger Canyon
- **Recommended categories:** HVAC, tutoring/test prep, family dentist, pest control, orthodontics, med spa

## Profile: Pleasanton (94566 / 94588)

- **Tier:** 2
- **Character:** Established Tri-Valley suburb, high homeownership, Main Street + Stoneridge retail
- **Recommended categories:** Pool, landscaping, orthodontics, cleaning services, HVAC, dentist

## Profile: Dublin (94568)

- **Tier:** 2
- **Character:** Rapid growth, family-heavy, strong retail along Dublin Boulevard / Fallon
- **Recommended categories:** HVAC, pest control, tutoring, family dentist, orthodontics

## Profile: Los Gatos (95030 / 95032)

- **Tier:** 1
- **Character:** High-income foothill town; mix of estate properties and dense village retail
- **Recommended categories:** Med spa, luxury home services, pool, dentist, landscaping

## Recommended First Campaign

**Danville (94526)** or **San Ramon (Bishop Ranch corridor)** — strong community identity, HOA infrastructure, high homeownership, dense local business activity.
